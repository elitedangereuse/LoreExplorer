#+title: Gallant Investment Brokers
#+filetags: :Corporation:Federation:3301:

Federal investment and finance corporation whose vast financial holdings drove the rapid growth of the [[id:735218d7-87f7-4570-8585-9c5ab0288c1d][Exphiay]] system, under the motto "Finding the perfect investment." Its CEO, [[id:d699f206-6c66-4fc3-ba3e-1ce80104993f][Frank Hall]], was among the powerbrokers attending the 153rd Exphiay Bankers' Ball in 3301 alongside Shadow President [[id:02322be1-fc02-4d8b-acf6-9a9681e3fb15][Zachary Hudson]].
