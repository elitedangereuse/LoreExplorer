* Community Goal: Fifth Aegis Initiative

/24 Nov 3303/

[[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]] has announced a second military strike on a Thargoid-occupied system, following the success of Operation Andronicus. The operation will focus on [[id:9cdea6e2-2abe-43ef-b874-44c09310f310][Electra]], where high concentrations of Thargoid vessels have been reported.

Admiral [[id:7bca1ccd-649e-438a-ae56-fb8ca34e6440][Aden Tanner]], [[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]]'s chief military liaison, elaborated on the nature of the operation:

"We have two primary objectives: To reduce the number of Thargoid vessels operating in [[id:9cdea6e2-2abe-43ef-b874-44c09310f310][Electra]], and to gather Thargoid material with which to test and calibrate our anti-Thargoid technologies."

[[id:3808ab0e-fcff-4299-b882-f8c3946f272d][Aegis Research]] has promised to reward pilots who hand in Pilots [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] [[id:bbbc7bc6-79d7-46b7-925e-c1f882c8f25a][Combat Bonds]] at Cavaleiri in the [[id:9cdea6e2-2abe-43ef-b874-44c09310f310][Electra]] system.

The campaigns begin on the 23rd of November and will run for one week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end immediately.
