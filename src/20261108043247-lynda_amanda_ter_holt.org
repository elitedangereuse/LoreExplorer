#+title: Lynda Amanda ter Holt
#+filetags: :Individual:3312:

Chief expedition astronomer of the [[id:b1b57ce2-5c91-46b6-87aa-c3f1fa29838c][Third Distant Worlds Expedition]]. In 3312, after the fleet departed [[id:da49a702-3bb4-4dd7-9adf-29188adb2801][Beagle Point]], she coordinated the expedition’s survey of sparsely catalogued regions of the galaxy, gathering high-resolution stellar data.
