[[id:1c4d53a2-d81f-4cfa-8001-eb77f2c79b5f][The Alliance]] has announced plans to build an [[id:5700c476-8bf4-4710-90d9-1e5988bf6048][Ocellus]] starport in the MEL
22 Sector GM-V C2-8 system, to further bridge the gap between the core
systems and the [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]]'s outposts in the [[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]].

[[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] official [[id:eb46eb3f-c399-45fa-a0e3-8362595e24ee][Torvan Dast]] issued the following statement:
Our previous initiative established a link between our outposts in the
[[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]] and the galactic core. But to ensure the safety and
wellbeing of travellers following the route, we need to maintain a
robust programme of starport construction.

The initiative is being overseen by the [[id:f76ba965-89ab-484f-8e67-b1996174a4a8][Turner Research Group]], which has
placed a kill order on all ships on its wanted list to allow those
delivering materials for the project to do so in safety.
