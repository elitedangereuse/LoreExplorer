Security forces in [[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] have recently found themselves coming under
considerable assault by a group of criminals.

Exact details as to the nature of the [[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] Jet Gang business remains
unclear, although source close to [[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] Security have indicated that
the criminal faction is known to be heavily involved in extortion. In
order to provide local security services with additional the manpower
they need to combat the [[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] Jet Gang, the Silver United Solutions are
currently looking to employ a number of bounty hunters to help bolster
[[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] Security.

Any bounty huner who takes part in the ongoing security initiative wil
be eligible for bonus payments from the Silver United Solutions once the
fight against the [[id:4c65480c-a87b-421b-a91a-f1f1f8ecb737][Zaonce]] Jet Gang has come to a close.
