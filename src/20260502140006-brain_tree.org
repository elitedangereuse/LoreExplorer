#+title: Brain Tree
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

Brain Trees are a species of Fungal Life that have been found on the
surfaces of airless planets and moons across the galaxy. They
resemble Earth-like trees with a brain-shaped structure at the end
of its branches. They are resilient organisms that absorb minerals
via their subsurface roots and energy via their outer skin.
