* Community Goal: Conflict Continues in [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]]

/11 Nov 3302/

Early reports from the [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] system indicate that the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] has delivered a significant blow to the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]]. The news comes less than 24 hours after the criminal organisation launched an operation to push the PRE out of [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]].

It is understood the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] leveraged its connections in [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]]'s criminal network to achieve its aims in a remarkably short amount of time.

But the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] is not out of the fight yet. The organisation has placed a kill order on all wanted ships operating in [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] and the surrounding systems, and has promised to reward pilots who deliver bounty vouchers to [[id:4223b99a-7e0c-44fa-b7e9-afeca7e0c031][Obsidian Orbital]].

The campaign will run from the 11th to the 17th of November 3302. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end immediately.
