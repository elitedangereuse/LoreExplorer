HORIZONS REQUIRED

Recent reports from the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]]-controlled [[id:5fcd46ca-b9bc-4135-8596-79ac2f8883f5][Pleiades Sector IH-V C2-16]]
system indicate that the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]], a Federal
organisation, is preparing to launch an assault with the aim of taking
the region from the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]].

A spokesperson for the [[id:c09feece-7689-4caa-bb83-dbde4757f6b0][Imperial Inquisition]], which is coordinating the
Imperial resistance, said:

"I would not surrender so much as an inch of territory to the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]]
-- I am certainly not about to surrender the [[id:5fcd46ca-b9bc-4135-8596-79ac2f8883f5][Pleiades Sector IH-V C2-16]]
system. I hope the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] is prepared for a
fight."

The [[id:c09feece-7689-4caa-bb83-dbde4757f6b0][Imperial Inquisition]] has asked all [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]]-aligned pilots to travel
to [[id:5fcd46ca-b9bc-4135-8596-79ac2f8883f5][Pleiades Sector IH-V C2-16]] and help repel the Federal offensive.

Both factions have set out a week-long operation for control of the
system, which will begin on the 11th of August 3302.

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before handing in [[id:bbbc7bc6-79d7-46b7-925e-c1f882c8f25a][Combat Bonds]] at TII Research Facility in [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]]
Sector IH-V C2-16 system.

Both factions have set out week-long operations to take control of the
system, but if either side is defeated before the end of the week the
conflict will end immediately.
