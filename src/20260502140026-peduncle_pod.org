#+title: Peduncle Pod
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These pods contain the seeds of the peduncle tree, a space-based
organism with some of the qualities of a terrestrial plant.
#+end_quote

Peduncle Pods are spacefaring plant-like organisms produced by
[[id:166d3e6c-b789-4135-b1e6-1e0a0ca89719][Peduncle Trees]] and found among Notable Stellar Phenomena. They move
in random directions by expelling a green liquid. They are sometimes
located at the same sites as [[id:166d3e6c-b789-4135-b1e6-1e0a0ca89719][Peduncle Trees]], but both organisms do
not necessarily share the same type; for example, Gypseeum
Peduncle Pods have been reported near Caeruleum
[[id:166d3e6c-b789-4135-b1e6-1e0a0ca89719][Peduncle Trees]].
