#+title: Falchion
#+filetags: :3302:Player:Individual:

Falchion is a Pilots Federation commander who won the Melee Masters
arena competition in 3302. Reports noted that the victory was secured
through a feigned surrender in the final bout.

In /Elite Dangerous: Premonition/, Falchion is confirmed as the
callsign/alias of [[id:4ab3e632-de21-44bc-a834-83b808a737ec][Raan Corsen]].
