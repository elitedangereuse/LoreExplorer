* Powerplay Standings: July 3311

/15 Jul 3311/

This month’s analysis of the influence held by key figures in the core systems has arrived, courtesy of [[id:8a3f6489-2b06-4494-89ce-5094392c7a99][Conrad Sterling]] of [[id:4ab0f53c-0b85-43a3-83ca-b9e88c0db30e][Vox Galactica]].

His latest survey of system populations suggests the following five powers enjoy the largest sphere of influence:

Rank 1: [[id:b402bbe3-5119-4d94-87ee-0ba279658383][Aisling Duval]] - 1,661 systems

Rank 2: [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]] - 1,581 systems

Rank 3: [[id:34f3cfdd-0536-40a9-8732-13bf3a5e4a70][Arissa Lavigny-Duval]] - 1,480 systems

Rank 4: [[id:b4892958-b513-46dc-b74e-26887b53f678][Yuri Grom]] - 1,114 systems

Rank 5: [[id:f0655b3a-aca9-488f-bdb3-c481a42db384][Li Yong-Rui]] - 1,018 systems

The largest increase in system influence belonged to [[id:b402bbe3-5119-4d94-87ee-0ba279658383][Aisling Duval]] who can claim an additional 104 systems as supporters.

[[id:4ab0f53c-0b85-43a3-83ca-b9e88c0db30e][Vox Galactica]] will provide another update on the state of the powers next month.
