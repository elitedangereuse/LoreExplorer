#+title: Crystalline Shard
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These crystalline structures are created by large colonies of
microorganisms.
#+end_quote

Crystalline Shards are crystalline structures found on airless
planets and moons. They are produced by colonies of microorganisms,
and are known to feature Crystalline Clusters that are rich in high-
grade Raw Materials.
