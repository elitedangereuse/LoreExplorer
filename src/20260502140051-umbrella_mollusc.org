#+title: Umbrella Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
These organisms, so called because of their resemblance to
umbrellas, are protected by husks that shield them from radiation.
#+end_quote

Umbrella Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that
inhabit Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs
native to Earth.
