The [[id:360ae21e-63f2-43ba-a2fd-a47e5e49951e][Cemiess]] [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] Party has issued an open contract for Exploration
Data on behalf of the [[id:01980efc-1b06-4f55-bf18-fa6c7e56f1eb][Imperial Internal Security Service]]. According to
the IISS, the data will be used to locate bases belonging to the
insurgent organization Emperor's Dawn.

To help with this effort, the [[id:360ae21e-63f2-43ba-a2fd-a47e5e49951e][Cemiess]] [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] Party is offering a series
of incentives to pilots who deliver Exploration Data to Mackenzie Relay
until such time as targets have been met.
