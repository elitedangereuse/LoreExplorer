The [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] is fighting to prevent further factions from declaring
independence in the Kuwembaa system.

[[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] has broken ties with the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] in protest
against the government's civil surveillance programme. Governor Sonal
Mishra, the leader of [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]], has requested assistance from
the galactic community:

"The [[id:5bd5ffaf-9210-4ee4-a1ef-46228d431c11][Federal Accord]] is no longer fit for purpose. We require support to
make a stand against this violation of our constitutional rights."

Three other Federal factions - Selkana Labour, United HIP 108095 Values
Party and Mutumu Future - are considering becoming independent entities,
depending on the result of the Kuwembaa conflict. These factions
currently control 28 systems in total.

[[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] has offered credit rewards to any pilots willing to
help secure its independence by handing in [[id:bbbc7bc6-79d7-46b7-925e-c1f882c8f25a][Combat Bonds]] at Gagnan Port
in the Kuwembaa system.

Should [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] match tiers with Li Chotep for Equality, its
independence will be confirmed.
Should [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] end one tier ahead of Li Chotep for Equality,
then Selkana Labour will declare independence.
Should [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] end two tiers ahead of Li Chotep for
Equality, then United HIP 108095 Values Party will also declare
independence.
Should [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] end three tiers ahead of Li Chotep for
Equality, then Mutumu Future will also declare independence.

The two factions have set out seven-day campaigns, which will end when
the top tier is reached by either faction or early on Thursday the 12th
of August 3307.
