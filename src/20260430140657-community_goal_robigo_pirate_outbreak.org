The [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] have issued a system wide kill order to protect
its new mining operations in the Robigo system. In a statement a [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]]
Corporation representative said:
"These opportunists are using the expansion of our mining operations to
attempt to get a foothold in the system, We cannot allow this and they
must be stamped out."

The [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] are offering a significant reward for any
bounties from the elimination of the pirate elements plaguing the
system.
