Location = Eol Prou LW-L C8-76 \[[[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] Dream\]
([[id:dfcd3273-3589-4725-a75f-5f1a08adc662][Coriolis]] Starport, large pads available, 26 Ls from main star,
21,997.12 Ly from [[id:6ace5ab9-af2a-4ad7-bb52-6059c0d3ab4a][Sol]])

It has now been eight months since the first [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] outpost was
established, and since then the colony has gone from strength to
strength.

With the [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] community continuing to expand, the [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]]
Co-operative has placed an open order for mined [[id:19346f59-fa1a-4ba2-8373-5b4d90f26662][Gallite]], Gold and
[[id:59d1161f-98db-4a4d-a0f7-b55a742a7ed3][Methanol Monohydrate Crystals]] for the development of infrastructure
throughout [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] and the surrounding systems.

A spokesperson for the [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] Co-operative released a statement:

"It would be impossible to overstate how many resources are needed to
sustain and grow the [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] community. Suffice it to say that we are in
constant need. Fortunately, thanks to a series of corporate donations,
we are in a position to offer generous premiums to pilots who deliver
materials to [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] Dream in the Eol Prou Lw-L C8-76 system."

The campaign begins on the 13th of April 3303 and will run for one week.
If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end
immediately.
