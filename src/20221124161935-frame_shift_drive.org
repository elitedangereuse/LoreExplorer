#+title: Frame Shift Drive
#+filetags: :3312:Thargoid:

[[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] introduced the Frame Shift Drive to the market in 3297.

A possible connection to Thargoid technology remains unconfirmed.

The earlier [[id:4893bb15-53b4-42b5-8f27-e3f6f5d7f165][Hybrid Drive]] research at [[id:5caecb15-c8eb-4f3e-8856-db3dd116c017][Almeida Landing]] is a potential lead, but no established source confirms a connection.

In 3312, [[id:4a28463f-cbed-493b-9466-70cbc6e19662][Core Dynamics]] announced that its [[id:e8a1cc47-c48a-4269-8ee1-726757a9547e][Kestrel]] combat ship would be fitted with supercharged Frame Shift Drives.
