In recent weeks, both the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] and the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] have accelerated
their expansion plans in the [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] and [[id:70fa34ea-bc98-40ff-97f0-e4f4538387a6][Merope]] systems, deploying dozens
of ships and establishing a number of outposts. The latest phase of
these campaigns will see both superpowers constructing bases in the
[[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] region.

[[id:1047d7bb-a08d-430d-a445-4ec9a1b2daa3][The Empire]] has authorised the [[id:e8740636-76cb-40ad-816e-ec17e1323aa0][Merope Expeditionary Fleet]] to coordinate
its campaign, while the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] has agreed to
oversee the Federal appeal. Both organisations have placed open orders
for construction materials, promising to reward pilots who deliver
[[id:d10c0435-4bc8-42ac-bea6-228d2f02604c][Tantalum]], [[id:0f1939e4-1ab1-4f9c-8b73-ebc572081433][Building Fabricators]] and [[id:3392ce5d-db80-4085-8821-57b2ec3ddf36][Thermal Cooling Units]] to their
respective bases of operation -- PRE Logistics Support Gamma for the
Federal campaign and [[id:91d67012-ad47-4b75-aac1-dba7c70e671c][Moni's Hub]] for the Imperial initiative.

Both campaigns begin on the 25th of May and will run for one week. If
either the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] or the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] meets its targets earlier than
planned, its campaign will end immediately.

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before delivering [[id:d10c0435-4bc8-42ac-bea6-228d2f02604c][Tantalum]], [[id:0f1939e4-1ab1-4f9c-8b73-ebc572081433][Building Fabricators]], and Thermal Cooling
Units to [[id:91d67012-ad47-4b75-aac1-dba7c70e671c][Moni's Hub]] in the [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] system.

Be aware that faction-state changes, UA bombing and other disruptive
events can negatively impact markets and station services, and could
prevent the initiative from running smoothly.
