#+title: Peduncle Tree
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organic structures are characterised by their distinctive
stalk-like limbs.
#+end_quote

Peduncle Trees are spacefaring, plant-like organisms that produce
Peduncle Pods and are found among Notable Stellar Phenomena.
Different types of Peduncle Trees have varying numbers of limbs and
are coloured a different shade ranging from cream to brown.
