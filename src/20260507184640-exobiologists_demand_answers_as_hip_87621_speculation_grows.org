* Exobiologists Demand Answers as HIP 87621 Speculation Grows

/25 Sep 3311/

Leading voices in the exobiological community are demanding clarity on the fate of [[id:959e9163-c6dc-41ae-913f-67f0a317c53f][Terri Tora]], the young researcher who recently went missing around [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]].

[[id:1d6960e0-ee47-47da-bddd-03f1f1f9aa35][Professor Frank Raddix]], who originally appealed for help in locating his young partner, issued a new statement this week:

“Terri risked everything in pursuit of discovery. We deserve to know whether she is safe and what exactly she found. Continued silence from the authorities is unacceptable.”

Independent journalist [[id:ff8d15ee-a8b7-4883-b850-5474bddd20ba][Alexei Keel]] of [[id:4ab0f53c-0b85-43a3-83ca-b9e88c0db30e][Vox Galactica]] has published evidence linking the permit lock to billionaire investor [[id:63f2962b-371f-4475-bbd1-0314201d15c0][Lexi October]]. [[id:1133e15f-59dd-42b3-aaa9-12a0bf3a3287][October Consortium]], which predominantly deals in technology, gambling circles, and high-profile investments, refused to comment when approached. Lexi’s silence has fuelled intense speculation over whether her organisation is involved with controlling access to the system.

Keel noted that the [[id:1133e15f-59dd-42b3-aaa9-12a0bf3a3287][October Consortium]]’s resources would make it well-placed to secure influence over a site of potential scientific importance. Many now speculate that [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]] contains valuable discoveries that have been secured for private interests.

Keel also pressed representatives of [[id:77cf2f14-105e-4041-af04-1213f3e7383c][the Empire]], [[id:d56d0a6d-142a-4110-9c9a-235df02a99e0][the Federation]], and [[id:1d726aa0-3e07-43b4-9b72-074046d25c3c][the Alliance]], and while each denied any connection to [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]]’s permit or the whereabouts of [[id:959e9163-c6dc-41ae-913f-67f0a317c53f][Terri Torra]], he did note that they were following developments closely.
