The [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] has issued a request for [[id:89bb247d-d459-4ebf-a000-698cd1d9c5fe][Osmium]] in order to
elevate the economy of [[id:da376681-8c08-4409-9565-3b91330fce01][HIP 8396]]. The project is the first of a series of
initiatives designed to boost systems recently brought under [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]]
Corp. control. To assist with this effort, [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] is
offering a series of incentives to pilots who deliver [[id:89bb247d-d459-4ebf-a000-698cd1d9c5fe][Osmium]] to Stepping
Stone Base until such time as targets have been met.

(NOTE: Turn-in is an outpost, so medium-sized ships or smaller only!
Current closest pristine metallic rings = HIP 4318, about 45Ly away from
[[id:da376681-8c08-4409-9565-3b91330fce01][HIP 8396]]. Redditors in the Elite Mining subreddit are also reporting
high amounts of [[id:89bb247d-d459-4ebf-a000-698cd1d9c5fe][Osmium]] in the A ring of the planet Stepping Stone
orbits.)
