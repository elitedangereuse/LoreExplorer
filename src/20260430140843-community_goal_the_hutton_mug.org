A company based in [[id:2de7ac5c-9ae0-4ebf-af7d-6505f05d2fd1][Alpha Centauri]] is producing commemorative mugs for
those who complete the so-called 'Hutton run' - the 0.22 light-year
trek from the system's star to [[id:9e3161ac-9252-44d0-a526-b6e289cf28ee][Hutton Orbital]]. The mugs, which bear the
legend 'I made it to [[id:9e3161ac-9252-44d0-a526-b6e289cf28ee][Hutton Orbital]]', are made from [[id:639c7c13-7a10-46b4-b2de-82db57364db1][Scrap]] material,
and the company is in need of additional material with which to produce
their next batch. To help with this effort, the [[id:2de7ac5c-9ae0-4ebf-af7d-6505f05d2fd1][Alpha Centauri]] Freedom
Party is offering a series of incentives to pilots who deliver [[id:639c7c13-7a10-46b4-b2de-82db57364db1][Scrap]] to
[[id:9e3161ac-9252-44d0-a526-b6e289cf28ee][Hutton Orbital]] until such time as targets have been met.
