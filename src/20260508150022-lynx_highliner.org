#+title: Lynx Highliner
#+filetags: :ship:

Expanding into the passenger liner market, [[id:d24ee8f5-e2ec-4c71-b5fa-afc2d9710141][Zorgon Peterson]] presents
the Lynx Highliner. This medium-sized vessel has considerable
passenger cabin capacity potential, a notable jump range and good
straight-line speed. The Lynx Highliner is also surprisingly robust,
with solid defences offering protection from external threats.

The Lynx Highliner has been designed for medium-size landing pads.

* Shipyard Stats
- Fighter bay: No
- Multicrew: +1
- Landing pad size: MED
- Speeds: 293 m/s top, 360 m/s boost
- Maneuverability: 3
- Jump range: 15.7 ly laden, 16.61 ly unladen
- Durability: 228 shields, 630 armour
- Hull mass: 260 t
- Cargo capacity: 32 t
- Dimensions: 18.8 m high, 89.3 m long, 45.2 m wide
