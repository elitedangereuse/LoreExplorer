#+title: Synuefe EN-H d11-96
#+filetags: :System:

System in Guardian space that became a hub of Guardian-human technological research in 3305. It hosted [[id:67e7d6ef-9eaa-4736-a9c5-a4a1fee4b100][The Prospect]] and [[id:701ff369-4602-43b0-a85c-56b2d3e31c85][Zende Partners Transport Service Operational]], and was later contested by [[id:60dabc80-ace0-4061-b21b-70ccfe37de12][Zende Partners]] and [[id:509c2dd7-f45e-4d6d-aa33-4ea3ca780652][Segnen Exchange]].
