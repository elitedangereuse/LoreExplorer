* Mahon Gains Advantage in Alliance Trade Boom

/05 Jun 3311/

An aggressive trade war in [[id:1d726aa0-3e07-43b4-9b72-074046d25c3c][Alliance]] space appears to have been won by supporters of [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Mahon]].

The recent withdrawal of [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] from Allied military infrastructure created a significant opportunity for member state companies. The shipment of cargo to key locations has been used as a barometer of success between two trade blocs, formed around [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]] and [[id:0d664f07-640e-4397-be23-6b52d2c2d4d6][Nakato Kaine]] respectively.

After two weeks, the trade bloc favouring [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Mahon]] has been declared the winner by analysts and pundits. [[id:76d00760-e607-4a24-aec5-a630460b3f31][Irfan Karim]] of [[id:ad2baca1-f970-4308-8b07-78cd4a5a8fd0][The Alliance Tribune]] noted that the Mahon trade bloc is ‘likely to secure the biggest and most lucrative contracts over the coming weeks.’

Participants in the trade war can now collect their rewards:

Supporters of [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]]’s trade bloc can find their rewards at [[id:f1db8856-a4de-4cd3-9767-d5e583e5dffb][Bowersox Port]] in [[id:b9e28958-51d6-4c64-a112-3e84b8fdaf72][Winiama]].

Supporters of [[id:0d664f07-640e-4397-be23-6b52d2c2d4d6][Nakato Kaine]]'s trade bloc can find their rewards at [[id:7742cf0e-a818-4fb6-a4f8-9f9622d8efd8][Fuca Dock]] in [[id:08b61168-83c6-4ef7-a9fc-9d6ccdbf9a3a][Apura]].
