* Galactic News: MetaDrive Acquired by [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]

/22 Oct 3302/

Board members of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] Inc. today announced the sale of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] Inc. to the [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]. [[id:4a385f89-7be7-426b-aba4-1206a2e5f345][Rosaline Merden]], acting Chair of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]], was clearly pleased with the outcome. At a press conference, she said: 

"The board of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] wholeheartedly welcomes the security this acquisition brings. As part of the [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]], the [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] brand will continue to go from strength to strength." 

Reporters asked about the ongoing audit of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]], and also the fate of former employee [[id:4ab3e632-de21-44bc-a834-83b808a737ec][Raan Corsen]], who was reported missing a couple of weeks ago. 

"The audit has been successfully completed and all assets are in the hands of [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]," said Merden. [[id:4ab3e632-de21-44bc-a834-83b808a737ec][Raan Corsen]]'s employment was terminated prior to the takeover." 

Merden was challenged by a representative of the [[id:81738027-bf28-48e1-982e-001c2b404c83][Earth Defence Fleet]], who alleged that [[id:4ab3e632-de21-44bc-a834-83b808a737ec][Raan Corsen]] had in fact fled from [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] security forces and been killed in the [[id:5c4e0227-24c0-4696-b2e1-5ba9fe0308f5][Alioth]] system. 

"Mr Corsen's business in the [[id:5c4e0227-24c0-4696-b2e1-5ba9fe0308f5][Alioth]] system was his own," said Merden. "It has nothing to do with the [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]." 

The press conference closed with outstanding questions, but reporters managed to get a final word from [[id:d50ec05a-0ca5-497f-a783-21b79243832e][Commander MoonWeb]] of the [[id:81738027-bf28-48e1-982e-001c2b404c83][Earth Defence Fleet]]. 

"According to our intel, Corsen was pursued before crash-landing on a planet in the [[id:5c4e0227-24c0-4696-b2e1-5ba9fe0308f5][Alioth]] system. This was observed by [[id:9291d40d-51f7-46a8-b2ec-6408875ad95d][Commander Ashley Wilkinson]], a [[id:2c8da7ca-a8b5-4ea1-bd52-e38ae83a9654][Canonn Interstellar Research]] representative working with the [[id:81738027-bf28-48e1-982e-001c2b404c83][Earth Defence Fleet]]." 

"The specifics surrounding Corsen's demise are not known, but we have a transcript of his final transmission that points a finger squarely at the [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]."
