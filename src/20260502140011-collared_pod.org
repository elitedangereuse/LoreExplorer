#+title: Collared Pod
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organisms are so called because of their distinctive collar.
#+end_quote

A Collared Pod is a non-sentient, spacefaring entity that inhabits
Lagrange Clouds. It appears to be a plant-like organism with
multiple bioluminescent spots on either side of its body. While it
does not visibly react to light, if a ship approaches it, it will
constantly alter its position so that one of its sides is facing the
ship.
