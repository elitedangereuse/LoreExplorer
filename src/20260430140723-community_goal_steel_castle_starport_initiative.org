Two construction companies have requested materials to convert refinery
outposts into starports, to assist owners of fleet carriers who use
large vessels for tritium refuelling.​
​
[[id:e5315e3a-50ef-4f11-bd17-4bfca3607622][Dean Bradigan]], director of [[id:a0de9a01-d94e-43bd-8bd4-4c607c6c21e0][Steel Castle Inc]], stated:​

"For travellers who find the familiar sight of [[id:dfcd3273-3589-4725-a75f-5f1a08adc662][Coriolis]] stations
welcoming, here's a chance to build up to five more. We ask for
shipments of bauxite, gallite and rutile to be delivered to CQC Holdings
at [[id:6b178669-1c3f-4679-9b36-7f2d2aafd22b][Teng-hui Station]] in the [[id:443b4ffb-1082-4c42-8766-c9450ab8c3c5][Nagasairu]] system."

The number of starports will depend on the amount of materials
delivered. The target systems in order of priority are [[id:9a7a830a-ad70-42ee-a17d-018d139a3ace][Fjorgyn]], [[id:dda8257e-5c5a-411a-b465-0a7495a312c7][HR 4979]],
[[id:ed48c6fc-8220-4d47-a8a8-60ceada78f78][Wolf 294]], [[id:83c5b09c-39e6-43b2-bd44-6d088c28ccff][Lambda-2 Tucanae]], [[id:bfe14a03-95e2-435b-b53f-f50e326f79ff][Potriti]], [[id:6ca74a70-76a6-4d1e-8d37-ee663163eb92][LHS 4058]], [[id:f7819579-c6c1-4134-89c1-469ac0aa28c5][Chakpa]], [[id:fb62cb94-7aa7-4dae-a18c-c653e2fa73de][Gally Bese]], Eta
Cephei and [[id:8b0cb052-2774-40b2-8805-36fcf5da1ad6][Ninabin]]. If successful, upgrading will be done for the start
of October.​
