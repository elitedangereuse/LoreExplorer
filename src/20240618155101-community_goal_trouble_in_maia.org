* Community Goal: Trouble in [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]]

/27 Oct 3302/

Earlier this month, the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] entered [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] and rapidly started cementing its presence in the system. In response, the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] – [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]]'s current controlling faction – has launched an offensive against [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]].

With the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] having recently stationed a [[id:3146c49e-4aa8-4061-9ab2-1d9da2b0bff5][Farragut]]-class Battlecruiser at [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] A 3 a, there is speculation that the appearance of [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] represents an effort to bring [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] under Federal control.

A spokesperson for the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] gave a brief statement:

"The [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] is clearly trying to pave the way for a full Federal takeover of the system. Well, if they want [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]], they're going to have to fight for it."

The [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] has appealed to the galaxy's Imperial pilots for help, promising to generously reward those who support their operation. The [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] has issued a similar request to Federal pilots.

Both organisations have set out week-long operations, which are scheduled to run from the 27th of October 3302.
