* Panther Clipper Enters Full Production

/30 Oct 3311/

[[id:d24ee8f5-e2ec-4c71-b5fa-afc2d9710141][Zorgon Peterson]] has confirmed that the [[id:e6801f23-b419-47e6-abcc-6089d12ea4c1][Panther Clipper]] has entered full production, with deliveries now commencing to shipyards across the core systems.

Previously available through limited release, the [[id:e6801f23-b419-47e6-abcc-6089d12ea4c1][Panther Clipper]] is widely regarded as the largest and most capable civilian freighter in history and has already become a fixture of the galaxy’s expanding trade and [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] efforts.

A [[id:d24ee8f5-e2ec-4c71-b5fa-afc2d9710141][Zorgon Peterson]] spokesperson remarked:

“The [[id:e6801f23-b419-47e6-abcc-6089d12ea4c1][Panther Clipper]] represents the pinnacle of large-scale transport engineering, meeting the demanding standards of long-range hauliers, corporate logistics and [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]].”

“The vessel’s hallmark feature remains its immense cargo capacity, exceeding 1,200 tonnes when fully optimised, along with compatibility with the advanced Mk II cargo rack system. We’re delighted to make the [[id:e6801f23-b419-47e6-abcc-6089d12ea4c1][Panther Clipper]] available to all.”

The [[id:e6801f23-b419-47e6-abcc-6089d12ea4c1][Panther Clipper]] is now available for purchase at shipyards throughout the core systems. Its launch and initial rollout were covered in [[id:e785a361-0e4a-4d41-8ad9-10d35a188719][Zorgon Peterson Launches the Panther Clipper]] and [[id:de43a5f7-4e93-4a8a-932d-822370ede530][Panther Clipper Enters Service, Trailblazer Resupply Calls]].
