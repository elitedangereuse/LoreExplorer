* Community Goal: Breakfast at Tranquillity

/17 Nov 3303/

The people of the [[id:e7c94b38-cdfb-48da-a1c4-cdad10c3371a][Leesti]] system are not the only ones celebrating this week. The Guardians of [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]] have announced plans to host a food festival in the [[id:1f56101f-de1d-4b48-beb3-7531ac5f494e][Tarach Tor]] system, prompting criticism from Independent [[id:e7c94b38-cdfb-48da-a1c4-cdad10c3371a][Leesti]] for Equality, which is coordinating a similar event in [[id:e7c94b38-cdfb-48da-a1c4-cdad10c3371a][Leesti]]. The Guardians of [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]] have strenuously denied that the event is an attempt to upstage Independent [[id:e7c94b38-cdfb-48da-a1c4-cdad10c3371a][Leesti]] for Equality.

Guardians of [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]] spokesperson General Hunter O'Brien released the following statement:

"The 'Breakfast at [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]]' food festival will showcase the very best regional cuisine, together with libations from [[id:1f56101f-de1d-4b48-beb3-7531ac5f494e][Tarach Tor]]'s many fine vineyards and breweries. In addition to these delicacies, we are hoping to be able to offer foods from the [[id:ff595332-6a13-4f69-ae2f-cc0a0df8e741][Lave]] sector of space, and have placed an open order for various rare goods."

In support of the event, the Guardians of [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]] have promised to reward pilots who deliver [[id:726ccb6b-cbbf-48ee-b1fd-73d56c186504][Azure Milk]], [[id:4aedfccd-a366-4b25-a5e2-538bb29a35cb][Diso]] Ma Corn, [[id:5efc49f5-465f-4dd8-afaa-b6bb3595a58c][Lavian Brandy]], [[id:38ca2282-281d-4561-bbaf-a617e378fd47][Uszaian Tree Grubs]] and [[id:fc39201e-c6b8-42f3-b2a7-d88fc564ee2a][Orrerian Vicious Brew]] to the [[id:d1fd2105-4b60-4892-8684-f18dde0c3097][Tranquillity]] starport in the [[id:1f56101f-de1d-4b48-beb3-7531ac5f494e][Tarach Tor]] system.

The campaign begins on the 16th of November 3303 and will run for one week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end immediately.
