#+title: Mudlark
#+filetags: :Specie:Lore:Nonsapient:

The mudlarks were a sentient species indigenous to [[id:bed8c27f-3cbe-49ad-b86f-7d87eacf804a][Achenar]] 6d,
which is known today as [[id:912f1172-e2b9-4fe9-9e53-13dd44fac4c4][Capitol]], the heart of the
[[id:77cf2f14-105e-4041-af04-1213f3e7383c][Empire]]. Tragically, the mudlarks were driven to extinction in
the early 24th century by bacteria carried by human colonists, though
rumors persist that Emperor [[id:a8a8c926-5bfc-4411-928b-7f88d2209e41][Henson Duval]] had deliberately wiped out
the mudlarks to make way for his colonial development plans. The
extinction of the mudlarks became the pretext for the
[[id:d56d0a6d-142a-4110-9c9a-235df02a99e0][Federation]]'s failed attempt to capture [[id:bed8c27f-3cbe-49ad-b86f-7d87eacf804a][Achenar]].

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.
