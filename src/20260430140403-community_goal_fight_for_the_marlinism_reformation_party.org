The Exiled [[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]] Assembly of [[id:e99db58f-3cfe-4548-b7da-284176167527][Baltah'Sine]] has declared war against
the Marlinism Reformation Party in the Hyades Sector RO-P b6-2 system.

Political relations degenerated into open combat following the NMLA's
recent attacks, with Acting First Minister [[id:eadb4e1d-bb5f-4f30-b0c6-b09243b66031][Amrita Ross]]'s efforts to
negotiate a peaceful resolution proving unsuccessful.

Supporters of the Marlinism Reformation Party are gathering at the [[id:5736dd4c-6645-4a96-b30b-7a56277decab][Steel]]
Majesty megaship. This was formerly used by the Neo-[[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]] Order of
[[id:bb9435b7-f21d-4afc-8ca6-a57d0fd51065][Mudhrid]] to flee from their home system.

Minister [[id:83ddd0cb-e22d-4586-bc11-692af4a335c8][Aaron Whyte]] sent this message from his private vessel:

"Volkov's unlawful strike is a violation of our colonies' charter. We
call upon all true Marlinists to defend us from this illegal act of
aggression."

[[id:1c4d53a2-d81f-4cfa-8001-eb77f2c79b5f][The Alliance]], [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] and [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] have informed the [[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]]
Consulate that they consider this conflict to be a strictly internal
matter.

The two factions have set out seven-day campaigns, which will end early
on Thursday the 19th of August 3307. If one faction meets its objectives
earlier than planned, the initiatives will end immediately.
