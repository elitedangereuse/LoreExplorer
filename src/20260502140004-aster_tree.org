#+title: Aster Tree
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organic structures are so called because of their star-like
shape.
#+end_quote

Aster Trees are massive plant-like organic structures found in
Notable Stellar Phenomena. Their limbs are tipped with [[id:7d84ffaf-8423-4917-87e2-9a33b3af6f05][Aster Pods]].
They are not trees in the traditional sense, but are long-lived and
extremely hardy space-based organisms.
