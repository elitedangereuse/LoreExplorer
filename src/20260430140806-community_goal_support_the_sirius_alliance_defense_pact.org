The [[id:25537b36-1d83-4354-afeb-482bf891d85d][Di Jian]] system is hosting competing campaigns as the [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]]
Assembly votes on the strategic defence pact.

The Assembly is evenly split over formalising military ties with [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]]
Corporation to enhance defensive options against the Thargoids, with a
decision expected at the end of June. In advance of the vote, both
pro-pact and anti-[[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] councillors are attempting to sway colleagues
to their cause by securing a sizeable resource package for the [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]]
Defence Force.

Pro-pact councillors have established their initiative in [[id:25537b36-1d83-4354-afeb-482bf891d85d][Di Jian]] from
the [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] megaship Kumiho Sky, and are asking for shipments of computer
components, reinforced mounting plates and synthetic reagents. These
councillors have heavily promoted the need for enhanced fleet support in
the face of increasing Thargoid activity. Noted engineer [[id:d18667b7-1da8-48ca-bb84-e280ebf77a35][Marco Qwent]]
recently confirmed his support for an Allied anti-xeno pact with
[[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]].

To encourage support from pilots, [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] is offering extra
incentives. The majority of contributors to the Kumiho Sky campaign will
receive a double-engineered AX fixed missile launcher, enhanced with
both High Capacity and Rapid Fire modifications, among other rewards.

Top 75%: 2B Double Engineered AX Fixed Missile Launcher
[[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] Permit
Top 50%: Additional 2B Double Engineered AX Fixed Missile Launcher
Access to [[id:d18667b7-1da8-48ca-bb84-e280ebf77a35][Marco Qwent]]'s Workshop
Top 25%: 3A Double Engineered AX Fixed Missile Launcher
Top 10%: Additional 3A Double Engineered AX Fixed Missile Launcher

The campaign will run until the 23rd June 3308. If the final target is
met earlier than planned, or the opposing campaign is completed first,
the campaign will end immediately.
