The Neo-[[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]] Order of [[id:bb9435b7-f21d-4afc-8ca6-a57d0fd51065][Mudhrid]] and the Epsilon Fornacis [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] Group
have declared war in the [[id:bb9435b7-f21d-4afc-8ca6-a57d0fd51065][Mudhrid]] system.

The [[id:5736dd4c-6645-4a96-b30b-7a56277decab][Steel]] Majesty megaship is serving as the base of the Neo-[[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]]
forces. These include many surviving NMLA terrorist cells that gathered
to overtake the vessel from the [[id:46536da8-abd4-461f-9bf9-b749d4fc25e1][Marlinist]] Colonies.

Also in the system is the [[id:04ae001b-eb07-4812-a42e-4bb72825609b][Far God]] cult megaship [[id:4701fafb-7040-4169-bfdb-9dc977a06e5f][Sacrosanct]], where the
NMLA's de facto leader [[id:7878ad2d-4118-4028-bfff-90a3976313bd][Theta Seven]] and the remnants of Theta Group have
been in hiding for months. They recently took control of the vessel and
are holding thousands of cultists hostage.

The Epsilon Fornacis [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] Group is orchestrating its campaign from
Payne-Scott City starport. Senator [[id:75daea85-5e9f-4f6f-a102-1a5edea0283c][Denton Patreus]] has requested that
Imperial auxiliaries provide the faction with combat support.

ACT's co-leader Captain [[id:ccaf380d-14e8-4a1a-9458-8c3bad87b25c][Saskia Landau]] stated:

"The NMLA clearly places huge value on [[id:7878ad2d-4118-4028-bfff-90a3976313bd][Theta Seven]]'s ability to
produce caustic enzyme weapons, and has risked concentrating in large
numbers to defend him. If he can transfer to the [[id:5736dd4c-6645-4a96-b30b-7a56277decab][Steel]] Majesty and
escape, the threat of further atrocities like the [[id:0713b7e5-a805-4b4d-91c0-104735484fbc][Nine Martyrs]] bombings
will remain high."

The two factions have set out seven-day campaigns, which will end when
the top tier is reached by either faction or early on Thursday the 2nd
of December 3307.
