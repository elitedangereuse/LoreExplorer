* Community Goal: Boosting the Federal Fleet

/04 Aug 3302/

As the antagonism between the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] and the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] continues to simmer, the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] has announced plans to expand its Navy with three new [[id:3146c49e-4aa8-4061-9ab2-1d9da2b0bff5][Farragut]]-class Battlecruisers. The development follows a recent initiative to add three new capital ships to the Imperial fleet.

In a statement, Federal President [[id:02322be1-fc02-4d8b-acf6-9a9681e3fb15][Zachary Hudson]] spoke candidly about the motivation behind the campaign.

"We live in uncertain times, and we are on the brink of an uncertain future. When I speak to the people of the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]], I want to be able to promise them that their homes and families are safe. By contributing to this campaign, you can help me keep that promise."

[[id:d56d0a6d-142a-4110-9c9a-235df02a99e0][The Federation]] has placed an open order for autofabricators, tantalum and superconductors for use in the construction, and has authorised the Workers of [[id:c981c9b3-05d4-421f-a167-3215666eadcf][Gooheimar]] [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] to reward pilots who deliver the requested materials to Brady Horizons in the [[id:c981c9b3-05d4-421f-a167-3215666eadcf][Gooheimar]] system.

The campaign begins on the 4th of August 3302 and will run for one week. If the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]]'s targets are met earlier than planned, the campaign will end immediately.
