#+title: Void Heart
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organic structures are remarkably long lived, with some
specimens enduring for thousands of years.
#+end_quote

Void Hearts, also called Barbed Knots, are plant-like organic
structures found in Notable Stellar Phenomena. They produce
Octahedral Pods, and are known to produce periodic bursts of heat
and light.
