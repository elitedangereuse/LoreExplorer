#+title: Quadripartite Pod
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These seed pods are characterised by their distinctive four-
chambered shape.
#+end_quote

Quadripartite Pods are seed pods found in Notable Stellar Phenomena.
They have a thick husk that protects their fleshy interior from the
vacuum of space.
