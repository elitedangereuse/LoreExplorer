#+title: Gnosis
#+filetags: :Megaship:Thargoid:3302:3303:3304:

Deep-space scientific-research megaship and flagship of the [[id:2c8da7ca-a8b5-4ea1-bd52-e38ae83a9654][Canonn Interstellar Research]] Group. Funded by a community goal that gathered indium, computer components and tea at Zoline Port in LTT 377, it was the first player-faction-owned megaship. The Gnosis made its maiden voyage in 3303, taking up position in orbit around Varati A6.

In 3304, while attempting a jump into the permit-locked [[id:52b032fa-2ac9-49fe-9240-49b9bf2ab3d1][Cone Sector]] FN-J B9-0 system, the Gnosis was hyperdicted by [[id:eba875cc-2486-46c4-8cea-7c6ff08f2386][Hydra]] Interceptors and left stranded near the eighth planet of the Outotz ST-I d9-6 system, surviving only because accompanying Commanders lured the Thargoids away with ejected [[id:da2f167d-0157-4deb-afb2-98bf6518cf01][Meta-Alloys]]. Its command crew was led by Captain [[id:ddd8a5bd-c70b-4239-b117-012617df1f03][Mathius Leander]].
