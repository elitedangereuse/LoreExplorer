Authorities in the [[id:8caa6742-32fd-41b6-972c-e5c7db882785][Aeternitas]] system have reported a sharp increase in
the number of criminals operating in the area. Reports indicate that the
agitators are attacking pilots travelling in the system, disrupting
trade and generally causing discord.

To counter this threat, the [[id:e751e6ab-6ad9-473e-8c59-597412ea57ba][Aeternitas Industries]] has placed a kill
order on all wanted ships operating in the [[id:8caa6742-32fd-41b6-972c-e5c7db882785][Aeternitas]] system, and has
promised to reward pilots who deliver bounty vouchers to [[id:c6e835f7-0645-4987-9fb6-5650617a5b1b][Houtman City]].

The campaign begins on the 8th of December 3302 and will run for one
week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will
end immediately.
