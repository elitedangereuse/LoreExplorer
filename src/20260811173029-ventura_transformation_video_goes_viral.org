* “Ventura Transformation” Video Goes Viral

/26 Jan 3312/

A health-conscious video recorded by holovid action star [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][William “Big Bill” Ventura]] has gone viral on interstellar networks, sparking a wave of positivity.

Known for comedic roles in traditional ‘real cast’ productions such as ‘Malacticus Runs Again’ and ‘For A Few Credits Less’, [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][Ventura]]’s notoriously vibrant party lifestyle has often made him a popular figure of fun and mischief in many celebrity gossip sections.

But a recent self-produced video of [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][Ventura]]’s workout regimen has exploded in popularity, with the actor showing a remarkable body transformation to lean, fit and strong.

“I’ve dedicated the past year to improving myself after growing unhappy with my image,” [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][Ventura]] told [[id:fae74250-4701-490e-9f25-86a70ad3a3d4][Corinne Macintyre]] of the [[id:ad2baca1-f970-4308-8b07-78cd4a5a8fd0][Alliance Tribune]]. “My energy levels have been through the roof, and after putting in so much effort I feel like I can achieve anything! I want to hike across the rainforests of [[id:ff595332-6a13-4f69-ae2f-cc0a0df8e741][Planet Lave]] in support of ongoing conservation efforts there.”

Messages of support and clips of people following [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][Ventura]]’s workouts have poured in, with millions of responses attributed to the original video. [[id:7a1709fa-42e7-477f-a402-a51092a55aa7][Ventura]] called the response ‘mind-blowing, just crazy to see so much love and healthy choices resulting from all this.’
