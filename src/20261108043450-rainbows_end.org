#+title: Rainbow’s End
#+filetags: :Station:3312:

Deep-space outpost built by the [[id:b1b57ce2-5c91-46b6-87aa-c3f1fa29838c][Third Distant Worlds Expedition]] logistics fleet in the Roefoo ZE-H d10-0 system, at the edge of the Mare Somnium region. Established in 3312 as a remote research hub to support long-range exploration and stellar cartography across the sparsely charted galactic northeast.
