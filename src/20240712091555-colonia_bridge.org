#+title: Colonia Bridge
#+filetags: :Infrastructure:Project:

The [[id:97a8c7e5-9399-4ebe-9457-8029ce809303][Colonia Bridge]] is a [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] infrastructure project designed to make travel between the core systems and the [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]] region easier and safer. The initiative established a chain of 56 stationary megaships and later added six permanent starports along the route between [[id:eb11ab9d-aab7-4d9b-aeaf-a228ef33d4da][Alcor]] and [[id:ba6c6359-137b-4f86-ad93-f8ae56b0ad34][Colonia]]. [[id:1e216de2-51f5-4f31-95cd-b37cb320ddc8][Twin Initiatives for Colonia Bridge Project]] [[id:d27c616d-04c9-4444-81cb-eb5803903f19][Colonia Bridge Reaches Completion]]

The first phases focused on placing dockable megaships every few hundred light years to provide refuel, repair and logistical support for long-distance travellers and fleet carriers. The final phase in 3308 completed the six starports and expanded their services to include facilities such as [[id:2fb00d7f-7234-45fa-8e3c-af685aa8f0d8][Universal Cartographics]], [[id:91b3e6e1-62aa-4d85-a93a-1d64765a8729][Vista Genomics]], outfitting and shipyards.
