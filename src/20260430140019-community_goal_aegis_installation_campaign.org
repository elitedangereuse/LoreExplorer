After the recent occupation of [[id:092e7139-1d8c-45d1-89ce-615326e10853][Socho]] by a hostile criminal element, it
is imperative that Aegis bolster its holdings in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector
IR-W d1-55. The construction of a military installation will allow us to
continue our fight against the Thargoids unhindered by external forces.
