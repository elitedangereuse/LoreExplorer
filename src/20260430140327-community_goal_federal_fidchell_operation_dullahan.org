The Sons of Conn have reported the presence of a small Federal listening
post hidden somewhere on the surface of [[id:5a007482-56ef-4f60-83e7-58b4089a0020][Lugh]] 6. It is believed that the
[[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] is using the hidden base as a way to issue secret military
intelligence to Federal forces operating in [[id:5a007482-56ef-4f60-83e7-58b4089a0020][Lugh]] space.

This flow of Federal information must be stopped!

Éamonn has issued a call asking for wings loyal to the Crimson State
Group to intercept all convoys as they attempt to leave the vicinity of
[[id:5a007482-56ef-4f60-83e7-58b4089a0020][Lugh]] 6. The Sons of Conn will offer assistance in any attacks, but only
for Commanders who pledge their allegiance to the Crimson State Group
before engaging the convoy.

Any examples of [[id:31d56d1f-c5af-4861-8c65-a47fd7c68dfe][Military Intelligence]] that are captured during the
course of such interceptions must be safely delivered to [[id:862a7049-e43e-419d-8e8b-dc72ac790f7e][undercover]]
field operatives currently operating behind enemy lines. If enough enemy
intelligence is captured, it should help weaken the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]]'s grip
on the system.

The intelligence already gathered by Crimson State Group supporters has
helped to fortify the defences aboard Crimson State Group controlled
outposts.
