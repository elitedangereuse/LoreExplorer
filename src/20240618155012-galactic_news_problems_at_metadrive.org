* Galactic News: Problems at MetaDrive?

/27 Aug 3302/

Independent auditor [[id:68956273-c433-4aac-aac3-1ebe826337e5][BigSix]] has reported various anomalies in the accounts of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] Inc., a company based in the [[id:2a4e7539-b87c-4ce9-8012-b2690fa3b618][Chi Orionis]] system specialising in hyperdrive research.

Reviewing the company's financial records, assessors found evidence of a series of payments to undisclosed suppliers but none of the corresponding checks and balances.

"It's a big mess," said the senior auditor of [[id:68956273-c433-4aac-aac3-1ebe826337e5][BigSix]]. "The company is being placed in receivership. It needs a buyer, and quickly."

[[id:9752d776-cd68-44a0-84fe-18132d8b5e74][Femi Dakarai]], the chairman of [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] Inc., was not available for comment.

Following the news, shares in [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]] Inc. plummeted. But a short time later, [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] unexpectedly volunteered to prop up the company.

"We've been interested in collaboration for a while," said the marketing manager of [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]]'s hyperdrive research division, "and were in talks over licensing [[id:f778f4fa-9cd0-4e2c-b3f4-6ee7adac4d03][MetaDrive]]'s exciting new technology. We have no desire to see them go under."
