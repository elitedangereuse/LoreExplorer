HIP Proto-Squid is a huge alien creature that has a strong
resemblance to the now extinct squid from [[id:5b0f485f-4793-468d-a1a1-483606f44e0e][Earth]]. It's also a specific
item of Foods in the galaxy of Elite Dangerous.

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.


Sold as a rare commodity at [[id:adf16b4f-60eb-4849-82ee-4e6d5401abed][Andersson Station]], [[id:cd7894ad-5bc7-4c15-b7c8-67120da11394][HIP 41181]].
