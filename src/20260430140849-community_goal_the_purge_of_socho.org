Following reports of Thargoid incursions into the core systems, the
[[id:79f218d0-1350-4c43-95f3-0869621dd59a][Aegis Core]] has launched a counter-Thargoid operation in the [[id:092e7139-1d8c-45d1-89ce-615326e10853][Socho]]
region.

A spokesperson for the organisation gave the following statement to the
media:
The goal is simple: Take out Thargoid vessels, gather up the pieces, and
deliver them to [[id:982f62a4-4f8a-44d3-a16e-0b87cde3327f][Dantec Enterprise]]. We don't know as much about the
Scouts as we do the Interceptors, so we want to compare materials from
both variants and find out what they're made of.

The [[id:79f218d0-1350-4c43-95f3-0869621dd59a][Aegis Core]] has appealed to independent pilots to support the
operation by destroying Thargoids in [[id:092e7139-1d8c-45d1-89ce-615326e10853][Socho]]. Commanders who participate
in the operation will be generously rewarded.
