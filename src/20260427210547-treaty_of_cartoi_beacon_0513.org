* 0513 Treaty of Cartoi
[[id:69c76cdc-f1e2-4399-96ce-4e050d4d425e][Cartoi]]

Commander [[id:8a4b4d05-f97b-418f-b3a5-f20416e9e11d][Lance 'Spacecat' D.]] invites you to visit Cartoi! In the weeks following Emperor [[id:6b07ceac-7fe6-43c0-98c9-460049eb1d7c][Hengist's passing]] in mid 3301 the backroom politics of [[id:77cf2f14-105e-4041-af04-1213f3e7383c][the Empire]] turned vitriolic. In particular the reace for the throne caused conflicts to erupt between followers of [[id:34f3cfdd-0536-40a9-8732-13bf3a5e4a70][Arissa Lavigny-Duval]] and those of [[id:b402bbe3-5119-4d94-87ee-0ba279658383][Aisling Duval]]. Cartoi was a system heavily disputed during that period, and was also where diplomacy won the day: The Treaty of Cartoi averted civil war and laid the groundwork for greater cooperation between Imperial powers in the months that followed.
