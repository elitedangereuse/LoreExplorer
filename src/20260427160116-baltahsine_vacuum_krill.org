#+begin_quote
Vacuum Krill are astonishing creatures. One of the few species to
live in the hard vacuum of space, they are native to the ice rings
around [[id:e99db58f-3cfe-4548-b7da-284176167527][Baltah'Sine]] 4b. Their eggs grow in vast numbers in tiny

Baltah'sine Vacuum Krill is one of the few known alien species to
live in the vacuum of space. They were discovered in the ice rings
around [[id:e99db58f-3cfe-4548-b7da-284176167527][Baltah'Sine]] 4b. They lay tiny pockets of eggs within icy
rocks. Secretions from the mother keeps the eggs liquid. Mature
Vacuum Krill can move between rocks with tiny fluid excretions.

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.
