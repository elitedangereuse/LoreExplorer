[[id:1047d7bb-a08d-430d-a445-4ec9a1b2daa3][The Empire]] has announced plans to bolster its Navy with a new Well-class
Carrier as part of its ongoing operations in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] star cluster.

The superpower has placed an open order for Silver, Micro-weave Cooling
Hoses and [[id:a081e7d6-7384-47d3-87d4-0c2324c9f3d0][Emergency Power Cells]], promising to reward those who support
the campaign. The [[id:e8740636-76cb-40ad-816e-ec17e1323aa0][Merope Expeditionary Fleet]] has been authorised to
coordinate the initiative.

The campaign begins on the 15th of June 3303 and will run for one week.
If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end
immediately

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before delivering Silver, Micro-Weave Cooling Hoses and Emergency Power
Cells to [[id:4223b99a-7e0c-44fa-b7e9-afeca7e0c031][Obsidian Orbital]] in the [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] system.

Be aware that faction-state changes, UA bombing and other disruptive
events can negatively impact markets and station services, and could
prevent the initiative from running smoothly.
