#+title: Bell Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
These organisms are so called because of the bell-like shape of
their shell. Despite being animals, they generate chemical energy
through a process similar to photosynthesis, using their tentacles
to absorb starlight.
#+end_quote

Bell Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that inhabit
Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs native
to Earth. When scanned with a Data Link Scanner, Bell Molluscs will
emit a green gas and move toward the ship, and when shot at they
will flee.
