#+title: Gourd Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
A gourd-shaped organism that feeds off starlight converted into
chemical energy.
#+end_quote

Gourd Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that inhabit
Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs native
to Earth. They appear to communicate with each other using whale-
like vocalizations. They also exhibit an interest in ships and will
follow them for several kilometers provided they can keep pace, but
will shy away if a ship approaches them closer than approximately 40
meters or they enter the vicinity of other objects such as Metallic
Crystals. When near a stationary ship, Gourd Molluscs will
repeatedly propel themselves toward the ship and then away from it.
