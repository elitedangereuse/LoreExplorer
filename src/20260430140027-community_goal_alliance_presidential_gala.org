The [[id:d17b2062-68b1-43ea-8852-ab04b591c6ba][Alioth Independents]] have announced plans to host a gala event in the
[[id:5c4e0227-24c0-4696-b2e1-5ba9fe0308f5][Alioth]] system, to officially begin the [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] presidential election.

The event will give the as-yet unannounced presidential candidates an
opportunity to meet distinguished figures from among the upper echelons
of [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] society.

Durien Ballas, chairman of the [[id:d17b2062-68b1-43ea-8852-ab04b591c6ba][Alioth Independents]], issued the following
statement:
Everyone's heard of President [[id:8520e75f-0479-42c5-9083-f9abfbad721e][Gibson Kincaid]], but the other candidates
may not be so well known. This will be their chance to shine. As well as
hosting key members of the [[id:48ac5ad9-dd0e-4d43-a109-f4cf6d3efdea][Alliance Assembly]], we can also confirm that
Prime Minister [[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]] and three members of the [[id:b0b347ac-10b8-4190-8787-1557f7d4a6da][Council of Admirals]]
will be in attendance.

To make this a truly spectacular event, the [[id:d17b2062-68b1-43ea-8852-ab04b591c6ba][Alioth Independents]] have
placed an open order for [[id:bc91675a-d24b-48f5-ace9-8229075afa6e][Esuseku Caviar]], [[id:0ca21b16-f85b-4e7c-9389-6196d4f52ef1][Ceremonial Heike Tea]] and Live
[[id:b3da0f0d-5c36-4fa0-8678-8c5ded220d61][Hecate]] Sea Worms, promising to reward pilots who deliver these
commodities to Smith Landing in the Amber system.
