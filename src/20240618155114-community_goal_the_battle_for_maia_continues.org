* Community Goal: The Battle for [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] Continues

/10 Nov 3302/

Two weeks ago, conflict erupted between the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] and the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] in the [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] system. But this was no ordinary inter-factional dispute. According to various sources, the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] was acting under direct orders from [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] officials eager to bring [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] under Federal control.

With both sides bolstered by Pilots [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] Commanders, the battle was intense and wide ranging. After several days of conflict it was the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] that emerged victorious, successfully gaining a foothold in [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]].

But it seems the battle for [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]] is far from over. Recent reports indicate that the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] is about to launch a retaliatory assault with the aim of pushing the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] out of [[id:0ee60994-364c-41b9-98ca-993d041cea72][Maia]].

The organisation has devised a two-pronged strategy to regain control, and has appealed to the galactic community for support. In addition to targeting [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] fighters, the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] has asked pilots to deliver battle weapons to system security forces, who will be supporting the [[id:c8cf3da4-154e-49a2-98ff-039aaab3b1dd][Ant Hill Mob]] during the conflict.

Pilots who participate in the operation will be generously rewarded.

The campaign begins on the 10th of November 3302 and will run for one week.
