An open order for military supplies has been placed on behalf of the
[[id:e9becd28-9644-42aa-afc8-7bba3ce10076][Imperial Navy]] by the Sirsir Co. A spokesperson for the organisation gave
the following statement:

The [[id:e9becd28-9644-42aa-afc8-7bba3ce10076][Imperial Navy]]'s logistics division has authorised us to process
deliveries of [[id:6f8b14ec-06ee-4f36-b646-15146a0ca81b][Battle Weapons]], [[id:85a9f73a-636f-459f-8a46-d93b1a3dca16][Reactive Armour]] and [[id:02df3938-8684-467d-b46e-cd60621e1fd3][Imperial Slaves]]. The
drop-off point is Mitchell Hangar in the Sirsir system.

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before delivering [[id:6f8b14ec-06ee-4f36-b646-15146a0ca81b][Battle Weapons]], [[id:85a9f73a-636f-459f-8a46-d93b1a3dca16][Reactive Armour]] and [[id:02df3938-8684-467d-b46e-cd60621e1fd3][Imperial Slaves]] to
Mitchell Hangar in the Sirsir system.

Be aware that faction-state changes and other disruptive events can
negatively impact markets and station services, and could prevent the
initiative from running smoothly.
