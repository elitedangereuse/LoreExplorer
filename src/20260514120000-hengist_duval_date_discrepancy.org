* Hengist Duval date discrepancy

The corpus currently preserves conflicting dates for [[id:3cb0755e-4deb-442b-898b-3f0c6651636e][Hengist Duval]].

- [[id:dc08846c-3427-4879-b556-ae3442c4ebba][Birth of Hengist Duval]] and the [[id:3cb0755e-4deb-442b-898b-3f0c6651636e][Hengist Duval]] codex entry give his lifespan as 3164-3301.
- [[id:d577bc0f-4fa1-4761-be27-7803508c1ffe][The Reign of Emperor Hengist Duval]] states that he was born in 3163.
- [[id:98b44118-c603-4868-ad86-a65c188f589d][The Line of the Duvals]] lists him as 3233-3301, which reflects his reign rather than his birth and death.
