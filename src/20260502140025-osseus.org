#+title: Osseus
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
The Osseus species grows a single thick stalk from which emerges a
wide, broadly circular, pitted endoskeleton. This structure is
designed to dramatically increase the surface area of the organism,
facilitating chemical capture and chemosynthesis on its
catalytically active surface.
#+end_quote

Osseus is a genus of alien organism found on planets and moons with
thin atmospheres across the galaxy. They can be sampled with the
Genetic Sampler and scanned with the Short Range Composition
Scanner. They have a clonal colony range of 800m.
