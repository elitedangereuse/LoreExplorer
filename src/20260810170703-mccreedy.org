#+title: McCreedy
#+filetags: :Individual:

McCreedy was a member of the xenobiology expedition at [[id:7e2f9cde-69fc-4351-b35e-236365ff178c][Botanical Research Station FNA-559]]. Exposure to spores from a local plant led to his death and the outbreak that overwhelmed the settlement.
