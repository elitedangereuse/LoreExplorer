The [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] are attempting to expand their control over the skied of
[[id:5a007482-56ef-4f60-83e7-58b4089a0020][Lugh]]. That cannot be allowed to happen.

If the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] wants a war, then the Crimson State Group will happily
oblige. Éamonn Uí Laoghire has called on all true allies of freedom to
gather together to show the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] that they will not be allowed to
maintain control of their subjects through means of oppression and
fear.

is offering sizable sums of credits to any mercenaries who agree to
fight on the side of an Independent [[id:5a007482-56ef-4f60-83e7-58b4089a0020][Lugh]].
