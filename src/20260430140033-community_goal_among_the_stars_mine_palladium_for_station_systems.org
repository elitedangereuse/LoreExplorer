* Objective

Help construction of a new station in the [[id:7e99a467-6e3d-418e-8b10-72cf3660efa6][Pleiades Nebula]] and earn
rewards by selling mined [[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]] to Neville Horizons in the [[id:3ceec3b8-48ce-40e3-8b24-ba6fe065d56c][Kaushpoos]]
System.

The latest reports from the Explorer's Association mention that
construction is underway on their newest [[id:5700c476-8bf4-4710-90d9-1e5988bf6048][Ocellus]] Exploration Station and
they are looking to move into the second stage of construction relating
to the stations internal systems.
The Explorers Association and [[id:2fb00d7f-7234-45fa-8e3c-af685aa8f0d8][Universal Cartographics]] are once again
teaming up to offer high premiums at Neville Horizons in [[id:3ceec3b8-48ce-40e3-8b24-ba6fe065d56c][Kaushpoos]] on
[[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]] mined from nearby systems.
A spokesperson for the Explorers Association has gone on record as
saying: "Our contacts at Brewer Corp, the contractor that won the
contract to build the new station have said that the more [[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]] that
can be sourced the better the systems that can be installed in the
station. The Explorers Association would like to make this new station a
shining example of what is possible with the latest technologies so we
are looking to all pilots to help deliver as much [[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]] as possible
to Neville Horizons."
Sign up as an official supplier and bring as much [[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]] as you can
to ensure the new station systems are top of the line.
