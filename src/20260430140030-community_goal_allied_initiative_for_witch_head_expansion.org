The [[id:ce73c3e5-6875-480c-95a9-c146f6247bb7][Witch Head Nebula]] has been confirmed as a primary source of
meta-alloys, following an initiative hosted by [[id:3808ab0e-fcff-4299-b882-f8c3946f272d][Aegis Research]]. The news
has triggered efforts to swiftly establish a human presence in the
area.​

[[id:3808ab0e-fcff-4299-b882-f8c3946f272d][Aegis Research]] released this statement from Professor [[id:c2623368-19b0-4995-9e35-b8f54f741a53][Alba Tesreau]]:​

"Having analysed large amounts of exploration data, graciously provided
by the galactic community, we can confirm that the [[id:ce73c3e5-6875-480c-95a9-c146f6247bb7][Witch Head Nebula]]
contains enough barnacle sites to meet the demand for meta-alloys. I am
confident that these sites will offer a suitable alternative to the
exhausted barnacles in the [[id:7e99a467-6e3d-418e-8b10-72cf3660efa6][Pleiades Nebula]]."

​[[id:1c4d53a2-d81f-4cfa-8001-eb77f2c79b5f][The Alliance]] has already dispatched advance teams to the region, with
the [[id:d17b2062-68b1-43ea-8852-ab04b591c6ba][Alioth Independents]] coordinating efforts to establish an Allied
presence in the [[id:ce73c3e5-6875-480c-95a9-c146f6247bb7][Witch Head Nebula]].​

Pilots seeking to contribute to this initiative should head to Judson
Station in the Mizar system between the 11th and 22nd of July 3305.​
​
To earn rewards you must sign up as an active participant before
delivering [[id:dcc20e2e-26dd-4106-b23b-4df951876173][Palladium]], [[id:94637eec-3dd2-478f-9f38-8930b96a15fe][Superconductors]] and Insulating Membranes to Judson
Station in the Mizar system.​

Be aware that faction-state changes and other disruptive events can
negatively impact markets and station services, and could prevent the
initiative from running smoothly.
