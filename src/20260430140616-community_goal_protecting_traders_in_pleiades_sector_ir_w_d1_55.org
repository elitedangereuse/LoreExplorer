Aegis has announced plans to build a military installation in the
[[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IR-W d1-55.

An Aegis spokesperson gave the following statement to the media:
After the recent occupation of [[id:092e7139-1d8c-45d1-89ce-615326e10853][Socho]] by a hostile criminal element, it
is imperative that Aegis bolster its holdings in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector
IR-W d1-55. The construction of a military installation will allow us to
continue our fight against the Thargoids unhindered by external forces.

[[id:3808ab0e-fcff-4299-b882-f8c3946f272d][Aegis Research]] has placed a kill order on all ships on their wanted list
to protect those delivering construction materials. They have promised
to reward pilots who hand in [[id:5402969f-345d-420c-9025-3a0a89929d11][Bounty Vouchers]] at The Oracle in the
[[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IR-W d1-55.
