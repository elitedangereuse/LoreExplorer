* Secure Resource Sites Declared in Dhan

/25 Apr 3311/

Independent pilots have helped eradicate large numbers of pirates in the
[[id:a21cd831-8c85-4efe-8ee0-e3e4d4d8eb9f][Dhan]] system.

Following an unusually high increase in piracy across the system’s many
planetary rings, local security fleets and skilled freelancers worked
to eliminate pirates and show criminals that it would be better for
them to relocate.

“Pirates are too foolish to give up a life on the wrong side of the
law,” said [[id:8fa8ad64-7ea9-4d02-aab1-5afb95c5ca53][Dhan Empire League]]
head of security [[id:e23331f8-cd14-4b8f-86a5-98f16ed89943][Harald
Gomes]]. “It’s all they know, and all they’re good for. But while we
can’t stamp out crime forever, we can at least send the message that
[[id:a21cd831-8c85-4efe-8ee0-e3e4d4d8eb9f][Dhan]] isn’t an easy target.
Attack our mining craft at your peril, ya lugheads!”

[[id:aa5d0177-2807-4e3d-b0d0-1a40b3203598][Gutamaya]], thought to have deployed
observers to monitor use of its recently released
[[id:e7e28c28-b97c-4106-9b04-8932279e267f][Imperial Corsair]] craft during
the conflict, is said to have been ‘very impressed’ by its craft’s
performance and the anti-piracy response force in general.

Participants that registered for the conflict can collect their rewards
from [[id:93f4e94e-b5b7-4e66-8a16-9fdbece1ad75][Kidd Station]].
