#+title: K-Type Anomaly
#+filetags: :Specie:Anomaly:

#+begin_quote
A range of high-energy phenomena characterised by luminous cloud
patterns.
#+end_quote

K-Type Anomalies are a form of Notable Stellar Phenomena. They can
damage ships that approach too closely or come into direct contact
with them.
