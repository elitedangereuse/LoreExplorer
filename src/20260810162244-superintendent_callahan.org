#+title: Superintendent Callahan
#+filetags: :Thargoid:Individual:

Superintendent Callahan managed [[id:2348a09d-4aec-4042-a2a6-db051f68d030][Betterton Outpost]] during its Thargoid
attack. His surviving reports record his attempt to evacuate the
colony and the escape of a group of survivors.
