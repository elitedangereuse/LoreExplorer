* Trailblazer Fleet To Be Withdrawn

/04 Nov 3311/

[[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] has announced plans to reassign its [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer]] megaship fleet, citing the self-sufficiency of newly established colonies.

The [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer]] vessels were deployed to provide logistical support and essential resources to independent [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] initiatives at the edge of inhabited space. Their presence has been credited with accelerating the construction of ports and surface settlements throughout the past year.

[[id:9b2b1a6d-3645-4776-aeee-4e2db897b988][Mark Rennik]], Chief Operating Officer for [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]], issued a statement of commendation:

“What began as an ambitious expansion effort has grown into a thriving network of independent colonies. The success of these initiatives proves that humanity’s frontier is now strong enough to stand on its own.”

“With these colonies self-sustaining and prospering, the [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer]] fleet’s mission is complete. Markets will close initially as we funnel [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] stocks that have supplied them to other projects. The ships will then jump to new locations at a later date.”

Withdrawal operations will begin Tuesday, 11th November, 3311. The fleet’s deployment and earlier activity were covered in [[id:8f6be64c-9cd0-479b-aa63-91c46282e2f7][Trailblazer Fleet Deployed to Support Colonisation Initiatives]] and [[id:dfa179f4-f41b-4dce-9799-99113689073b][Brewer Corporation Trailblazer Fleet Upgraded]].
