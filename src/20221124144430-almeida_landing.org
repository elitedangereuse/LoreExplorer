#+title: Almeida Landing
#+filetags: :DataPoint:Thargoid:Empire:Federation:Settlement:Abandoned:

Almeida Landing is an abandoned settlement in the [[id:52ce1ef8-7242-47fe-9735-68237061cb03][Conn]] system, on body [[https://edgis.elitedangereuse.fr/static/sysmap.html?system=Conn&body_id=7][A 3 a]].

The location of this facility was given by an [[id:1fd01d13-7a2c-402e-bb92-1b4edadd78c2][Unregistered Comms
Beacon]] that can be found in the [[id:ed9aa777-0465-41a3-a3db-c31549e55569][Teegarden's Star]] system.

The facility was operated by the [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][Intergalactic Naval Reserve Arm]]
(INRA), a co-operative between the Federation and Empire that operated
during the last war with the Thargoids. This facility appears to have
been used to reverse-engineer [[id:46a9c980-af48-4e43-a820-9971d7c76c34][Thargoid drive technology]].

The facility contains a number of agricultural tents, habitation
modules, and two communications towers. A couple of small tanks are
present, and while they are not marked with the symbol for the [[id:0ffe3814-d246-41f3-8f82-4bb9ca062dea][mycoid]]
[[id:6023377d-7271-49d1-80ec-ffab82dc8c29][weapon]], the ground is stained in a manner consistent with leakage of
the agent as seen at other sites.

* Related INRA Site

[[id:9ffffc4a-224c-42cb-b853-fd08d5c67e1b][Carmichael Point]] was the next INRA site in the discovery trail. Its
logs record an anti-xeno weapons test and indicate connections to
[[id:0ffe3814-d246-41f3-8f82-4bb9ca062dea][Mycoid]], but do not establish a direct link to Almeida Landing's
hybrid-drive research.

There are Engineer Materials scattered around the ground, and within
the facility there are two Data Points which may be scanned for an
Intel Package, and for Engineer Data; both need to be scanned within
30s.
