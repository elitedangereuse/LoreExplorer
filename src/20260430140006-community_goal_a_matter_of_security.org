For too long the [[id:187aedfb-f0ae-4d10-983c-c34d1122c842][Falisci Purple Gang]] have been terrorising the trade
routes between Dongzi and [[id:36dc081e-5308-4677-978e-cd9eee894d1b][Ngaiawang]], hiding behind legal technicalities
to avoid the sting of imperial justice. The [[id:b3659f96-e6dd-4c6e-b860-f3be6b6bcef1][Citizens of Tradition]] say no
more! No longer will we sit idly by while law abiding citizens are
attacked, beaten and robbed.

The [[id:b3659f96-e6dd-4c6e-b860-f3be6b6bcef1][Citizens of Tradition]] will bring [[id:80569507-5192-4d10-a072-7ddc2b0e5bac][Falisci]] to heel, but the glory
needn't be ours alone. Thanks in no small part to the generous support
of Senator [[id:75daea85-5e9f-4f6f-a102-1a5edea0283c][Denton Patreus]], a range of bonus payments (including the
usual spoils of war) will be made available to all pilots who sign up to
fight on the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]]'s side.
