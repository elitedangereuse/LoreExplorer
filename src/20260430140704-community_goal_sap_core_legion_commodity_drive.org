The Sap Core Legion faction has launched an initiative to build a
state-of-the-art medical facility in the [[id:48e6169f-7078-4a26-a8e8-e2e688238e55][Heike]] system. [[id:48e6169f-7078-4a26-a8e8-e2e688238e55][Heike]] has some
significance in the field of medicine -- a species of tea plant native
to the system played a vital role in the development of a cure for the
[[id:1389857c-3747-4cdd-ad3e-99b162df9308][Cerberus Plague]]. Sap Core Legion believes that this detail makes [[id:48e6169f-7078-4a26-a8e8-e2e688238e55][Heike]]
an ideal location for the new installation.​
​
Professor [[id:d0e95ed3-c443-495f-bad1-fff4d134f236][Macklin Fox]], speaking on behalf of Sap Core Legion, said:​

"Sap Core Legion wishes to build this facility in response to a number
of recent outbreaks and epidemics. When one also considers the current
Thargoid conflict, we feel humanity would benefit from additional
off-world medical resources."

Independent pilots have been invited to deliver [[id:5af29dcf-3079-46c2-a594-d2559c830744][Advanced Medicines]],
[[id:cf391eb1-f332-4044-9bc6-814ca24c52b1][Thallium]] and [[id:e6242e56-28c8-4db5-9c63-ba4dcbf11e1e][Medical Diagnostic Equipment]] to Braun Enterprise in the
[[id:48e6169f-7078-4a26-a8e8-e2e688238e55][Heike]] system.​
