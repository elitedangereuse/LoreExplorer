#+title: Thargoid Barnacle
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

A Thargoid Barnacle is an organic [[id:09343513-2893-458e-a689-5865fdc32e0a][Thargoid]] structure that extracts
resources from a planet and converts them into [[id:da2f167d-0157-4deb-afb2-98bf6518cf01][Meta-Alloys]], a key
component in Thargoid ships and technologies. The main structure
consists of an organic growth with a central, ribbed peak surrounded
by Thargoid Barnacle Barbs. The first [[id:b41c0ea3-93f4-4aef-8c9c-db0b140719c0][Barnacles]] were documented in
the [[id:7e99a467-6e3d-418e-8b10-72cf3660efa6][Pleiades Nebula]] and [[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]] in 3302, and additional
sites were discovered in the [[id:ce73c3e5-6875-480c-95a9-c146f6247bb7][Witch Head Nebula]] in 3305 and the
[[id:6f086af9-53a6-4d37-9800-b81d2537837c][Coalsack Nebula]] in 3306.
