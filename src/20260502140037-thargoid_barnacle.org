#+title: Thargoid Barnacle
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

A Thargoid Barnacle is an organic Thargoid structure that extracts
resources from a planet and converts them into Meta-Alloys, a key
component in Thargoid ships and technologies. The main structure
consists of an organic growth with a central, ribbed peak surrounded
by Thargoid Barnacle Barbs. The first Barnacles were documented in
the Pleiades Nebula and California Nebula in 3302, and additional
sites were discovered in the Witch Head Nebula in 3305 and the
Coalsack Nebula in 3306.
