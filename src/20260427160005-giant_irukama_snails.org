#+begin_quote
Despite appearances, the species discovered on [[id:76048bd8-7a18-4d77-bf77-ae23a5268d20][Irukama]] are not
strictly snails. These indigenous creatures almost destroyed
[[id:76048bd8-7a18-4d77-bf77-ae23a5268d20][Irukama]]'s native habitat over their sole predator was culled, due

surplus stock is sold on as a delicacy. The meat from a single snail

Giant Irukama Snails are a molluscoid species which resemble snails.
They're native to [[id:76048bd8-7a18-4d77-bf77-ae23a5268d20][Irukama]] and a food delicacy. The meat of one
Irukama snail can fill several standard cargo canisters. It's a
specific item of Foods in the galaxy.

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.


Sold as a rare commodity at [[id:0f682543-78e3-4181-bbb9-46b085deb122][Blaauw City]], [[id:76048bd8-7a18-4d77-bf77-ae23a5268d20][Irukama]].
