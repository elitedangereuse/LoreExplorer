#+title: Princess Astrophel and the Spiralling Stars
#+filetags: :Literature:

Princess Astrophel and the Spiralling Stars is a children's story whose details were said to resemble versions of the [[id:a2bd8247-2daf-4bd9-b6da-667ff707b0a2][Raxxla]] legend involving the [[id:865093c7-c399-460e-9dda-fb298f3f5d7f][Omphalos Rift]].
