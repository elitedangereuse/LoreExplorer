#+title: Cobra Mk V
#+filetags: :3311:ship:

Multipurpose ship manufactured by
[[id:273d7834-fe3f-4b12-b045-d5d8a62e719a][Faulcon DeLacy]]. Released more
widely in 3311 after an earlier limited release.

The Cobra Mk V is presented as the latest evolution of the Cobra line,
with improved handling, modularity, and enhanced compatibility with
[[id:bb129165-335b-4dc9-9cf0-ee6640728a92][SCO]]-enabled
[[id:46a9c980-af48-4e43-a820-9971d7c76c34][frame shift drives]]. It follows
earlier Cobra designs such as the [[id:83299a14-b6b0-4670-ba39-2914e05ed2f5][Cobra Mk III]].
