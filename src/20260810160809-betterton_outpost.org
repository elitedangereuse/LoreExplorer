#+title: Betterton Outpost
#+filetags: :Thargoid:Settlement:

Betterton Outpost is an abandoned settlement that has been attacked by
Thargoids in the [[id:6a270638-c107-42d5-b443-69c0b790ea4e][HIP 19792]] system on body [[https://edgis.elitedangereuse.fr/static/sysmap.html?system=HIP+19792&body_id=10][C 2]].

The location of this site can be found by following the three nearby
listening posts located in: [[id:51916839-dfae-46ab-874f-46ded08bc880][Pleione]] / [[id:203e2365-af25-465d-89f5-8ac3df7b9d3e][HIP 17892]] / [[id:7cb40dd8-9f5b-4b57-829d-91d53c96091f][HIP 19072]]

Things have become complicated over the last few days. I would have
submitted a report sooner, but I’ve been trying to quell a widespread
panic.
It started last week. Scattered reports of strange sightings. Then a
mining ship came in saying it had a ‘close encounter’. I laughed at
that…until I reviewed the ship’s security feed.

There’s definitely something out there.

I couldn’t do anything to contain the rumours. I’m not even sure I
wanted to. The people in this colony have a right to know. They have a
right to be protected. I’ve attached a full report, requesting an
investigation. We need assurances that we’re going to be protected
from whatever’s out there. I would appreciate it if someone would
respond to this request soon.

The facility came under attack before I’d evacuated even half the

Things? Might as well call them what they are - aliens. They bypassed

I’ve gathered together a group of survivors and we’re going to make a

military response. And I mean a whole fleet. Whatever these things

Who knows why it let us go. I can’t explain it. But this isn’t the
