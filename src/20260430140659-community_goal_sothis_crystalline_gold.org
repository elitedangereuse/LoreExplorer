Scientists based at [[id:15a6cb6a-a3c1-45b7-ae06-63c8e6755bca][Newholm Station]] in the [[id:aa43803c-e60c-45bf-ab48-49a139931c68][Sothis]] system have discovered
a new metalloid called [[id:488137ee-6e22-47f6-93b1-05f2a26e2780][Sothis Crystalline Gold]].

The material is produced by exposing ordinary gold to gas emitted by the
thermal vents lining the seabeds of [[id:aa43803c-e60c-45bf-ab48-49a139931c68][Sothis]] A5 and [[id:aa43803c-e60c-45bf-ab48-49a139931c68][Sothis]] A6.

The scientists of [[id:15a6cb6a-a3c1-45b7-ae06-63c8e6755bca][Newholm Station]] are eager to conduct further tests on
this new material, but to do so they will need more gold. To help with
this effort, [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] Unite! is offering a series of incentives to
pilots who deliver Gold to [[id:15a6cb6a-a3c1-45b7-ae06-63c8e6755bca][Newholm Station]].
