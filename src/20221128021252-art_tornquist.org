#+title: Art Tornqvist
#+filetags: :2296:Individual:

Art Tornqvist was a shipboard mechanic based in the [[id:000181d2-87fb-4eac-9c05-378082def97f][Tau Ceti]] system. His journal contains the earliest recorded mention of [[id:a2bd8247-2daf-4bd9-b6da-667ff707b0a2][Raxxla]].
