Namarii, Tabaldak and LTT 874 systems. Senator [[id:75daea85-5e9f-4f6f-a102-1a5edea0283c][Denton Patreus]], who is
spearheading the campaign against Emperor's Dawn, has dispatched
several squadrons of Imperial fighters to these systems. The Namarii
Movement, Tabaldak Imperial Society and the LTT 874 [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] Party have
been authorised to reward pilots who help fight the insurgents.
