Defence Director [[id:b95c418a-658c-41be-bfbc-939f73d1dfe1][Jacob Chowdhury]] said:
The Thargoid presence throughout the [[id:7e99a467-6e3d-418e-8b10-72cf3660efa6][Pleiades Nebula]] continues to be a
major threat. To reassure the public, [[id:db135581-804e-4f1e-9fce-ec2fe722e2c7][Atlas]] is investing in improved
defences for the [[id:70fa34ea-bc98-40ff-97f0-e4f4538387a6][Merope]] system.

To help protect incoming ships from criminal elements, we have allocated
bounties to all wanted vessels in the system.
