#+title: Parasol Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
These organisms, so called because of their parasol-like shape, are
protected by husks that shield them from radiation.
#+end_quote

Parasol Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that inhabit
Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs native
to Earth.
