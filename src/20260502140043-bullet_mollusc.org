#+title: Bullet Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
These organisms are so called because of the bullet-like shape of
their shell. Despite being animals, they generate chemical energy
through a process similar to photosynthesis, using their tentacles
to absorb starlight.
#+end_quote

Bullet Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that inhabit
Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs native
to Earth.
