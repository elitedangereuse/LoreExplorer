#+title: Odysseus
#+filetags: :ship:3312:

Flag carrier of the [[id:b1b57ce2-5c91-46b6-87aa-c3f1fa29838c][Third Distant Worlds Expedition]]. In 3312, during the expedition's passage toward Waypoint 13 in the [[id:edf930ec-8736-45c9-907b-4512626640a8][Empyrean Straits]], Odysseus was to install a commemorative beacon that would register the callsigns of scanning Commanders.
