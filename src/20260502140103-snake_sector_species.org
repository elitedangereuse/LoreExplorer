#+title: Snake Sector species
#+filetags: :Specie:Lore:Nonsapient:

The Snake Sector species is an unidentified species of predatory,
subterranean-dwelling insectoids native to the moon Snake Sector
GW-W c1-1 AB 5 b in the Snake Sector GW-W c1-1 system. They are
described as spider-like, but with more legs, solid white
colouration, a lamprey-like mouth, and a body size comparable to a
human fist. They also live in swarms of hundreds, possess rapid
movement, and consider all other heat-producing lifeforms to be
prey.

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.
