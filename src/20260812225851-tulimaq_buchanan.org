#+title: Tulimaq Buchanan
#+filetags: :3304:Individual:

Fleet Admiral and member of the [[id:b0b347ac-10b8-4190-8787-1557f7d4a6da][Alliance Council of Admirals]] who was assassinated by the [[id:f5c4ef7c-7a40-4d9f-87e1-904f11efda2f][League of Reparation]] in 3304. The murder prompted an investigation that later exposed [[id:315a2182-7266-45f9-ac87-5f0f42c5cf12][Riri McAllister]] as the League’s coordinator.
