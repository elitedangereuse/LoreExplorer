A cure for the [[id:1389857c-3747-4cdd-ad3e-99b162df9308][Cerberus Plague]] has been discovered, and the process of
eliminating the disease from the galaxy is now underway. Unfortunately,
however, the medical ships transporting the cure from [[id:24aa4f9c-26d5-4628-ba4b-13c370a13a77][Hart Station]] are
coming under attack from Pirates, who see the vessels as easy prey.

The [[id:1389857c-3747-4cdd-ad3e-99b162df9308][Cerberus Plague]] remains a significant threat, and it is of the
utmost importance that the antidote reaches the systems blighted y the
disease. The pirates targeting the medical supply ships must be
eliminated if the [[id:1389857c-3747-4cdd-ad3e-99b162df9308][Cerberus Plague]] is to be wiped from the galaxy. To
this end, the Union of [[id:ba9787a7-583f-4fb0-b9e1-3c0c0096b485][Bast]] Liberals is offering a series of incentives
to pilots who destroy these pirates. Interested parties should target
pirates with bounties from the Union of [[id:ba9787a7-583f-4fb0-b9e1-3c0c0096b485][Bast]] Liberals, and hand in the
bounties at [[id:24aa4f9c-26d5-4628-ba4b-13c370a13a77][Hart Station]] in the [[id:ba9787a7-583f-4fb0-b9e1-3c0c0096b485][Bast]] System.
