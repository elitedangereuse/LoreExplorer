#+title: Rhizome Pod
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These seed pods are so called because of their resemblance to the
roots of certain terrestrial plants.
#+end_quote

Rhizome Pods are seed pods found in Notable Stellar Phenomena.
Unlike other spaceborne seed pods, the parent organism of Rhizome
Pods has not been identified, and it is possible that they are
entirely self-contained.
