#+title: Whispering Plants
#+filetags: :Specie:Lore:Nonsapient:

Whispering Plants are alien carnivorous species from Quphieth. Plant
and animal are not exact terms to describe it. They're striking to
look at, pleasant to smell and fill the surroundings with an off-
Earth feeling.

Sentience: Non-sapient.
Location: Lore.
