* Community Goal: The Fight for [[id:5fcd46ca-b9bc-4135-8596-79ac2f8883f5][Pleiades Sector IH-V C2-16]]

/11 Aug 3302/

Recent reports from the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]]-controlled [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IH-V c2-16 system indicate that the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]], a Federal organisation, is preparing to launch an assault with the aim of taking the region from the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]].

A spokesperson for the [[id:c09feece-7689-4caa-bb83-dbde4757f6b0][Imperial Inquisition]], which is coordinating the Imperial resistance, said:

"I would not surrender so much as an inch of territory to the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] – I am certainly not about to surrender [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IH-V c2-16. I hope the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] is prepared for a fight."

The [[id:c09feece-7689-4caa-bb83-dbde4757f6b0][Imperial Inquisition]] has asked all [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]]-aligned pilots to travel to [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IH-V c2-16 and help repel the Federal offensive. Similarly, the [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] has issued a call to arms to all [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]]-aligned fighters.

The [[id:268dd81e-54f9-447a-9a47-11b2f7242e81][Pleiades Resource Enterprise]] has set out a week-long operation to take control of the system, which will begin on the 11th of August 3302.
