#+title: Kachirigin Filter Leeches
#+filetags: :Commodity:rare:Specie:Lore:Nonsentient:

#+begin_quote
Leeches were used medically for millennia on ancient
[[id:5b0f485f-4793-468d-a1a1-483606f44e0e][Earth]] but were largely superseded by technology. Hundreds of years
later, a chance discovery on [[id:2e2a8064-6ebf-4e7e-ba74-c5193a163fff][Kachirigin]] revealed some very valuable
properties of the native creatures. Kachirigin leeches can painlessly
filter impurities from the user's blood, so can be used as a cure-all
for hangovers and the worst effects of certain addictions. They are
prized by the wealthy and hedonistic, and have an almost cult-like
following.
#+end_quote

Kachirigin Filter Leeches are a Medicines Rare Commodity that is
produced at [[id:ea4a52c1-3e2c-4830-a88f-bbf40ed2e280][Nowak Orbital]] in the [[id:2e2a8064-6ebf-4e7e-ba74-c5193a163fff][Kachirigin]] system.
