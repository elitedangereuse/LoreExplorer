#+title: Sinuous Tuber
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organic structures are distinguished by their tubular shape
and vivid colouration.
#+end_quote

Sinuous Tubers are a species of Fungal Life that have been found on
the surfaces of airless planets and moons across the galaxy. They
were originally nicknamed "Tube Worms" by explorers due to their
resemblance to tube worms found on Earth.
