Smuggle illegal rare liquor into [[id:70fecaa7-bd23-4579-b206-7e3e65737044][Mansfield Orbiter]] in [[id:d40886f3-bc59-4f0d-8926-b125ee01c9bb][Epsilon Indi]] to
bribe officials in the fight to make [[id:ffcc8c23-1721-41cb-9cb7-c849292c8b38][Indi Bourbon]] a legal commodity.

Rumours from the [[id:d40886f3-bc59-4f0d-8926-b125ee01c9bb][Epsilon Indi]] system suggest that the United Epsilon
Indi Flag is recruiting independent Commanders for a campaign called
'Bribes 4 Bourbon'. In a series of illicit meetings with system
officials, the organisation reportedly secured an agreement to revise
the status of [[id:ffcc8c23-1721-41cb-9cb7-c849292c8b38][Indi Bourbon]] and make it a legal beverage.

Yulia 'The Needle' Kuriakova, a spokeswoman for the organisation,
said:

"For almost a year, authorities in [[id:d40886f3-bc59-4f0d-8926-b125ee01c9bb][Epsilon Indi]] have prohibited the
production of [[id:ffcc8c23-1721-41cb-9cb7-c849292c8b38][Indi Bourbon]], which is classified as an illegal substance.
Our plan is to bring vast quantities of illegal liquor [[id:70fecaa7-bd23-4579-b206-7e3e65737044][Mansfield Orbiter]]
to pay off the officials."

The United [[id:d40886f3-bc59-4f0d-8926-b125ee01c9bb][Epsilon Indi]] Flag has requested that [[id:221834bd-28f1-4d70-af5b-3d8558884da0][Burnham Bile Distillate]],
[[id:4b31a774-1f16-4b74-964f-df0460e058af][Eranin Pearl Whisky]], [[id:41fa0a3d-4f14-44c1-b314-78882dcc37b3][Leestian Evil Juice]], and [[id:eddbd97b-5374-4220-b6c0-26731c953c00][Wuthielo Ku Froth]] be
smuggled into [[id:70fecaa7-bd23-4579-b206-7e3e65737044][Mansfield Orbiter]]. A temporary black market has been
established to accept these commodities.
