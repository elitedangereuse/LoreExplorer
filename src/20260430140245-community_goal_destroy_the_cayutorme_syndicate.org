[[id:1047d7bb-a08d-430d-a445-4ec9a1b2daa3][The Empire]] has despatched a large military fleet to the [[id:3ae0f41b-9beb-419b-a804-68a000d9666b][Cayutorme]] system
to engage the mercenary group known as the [[id:3ae0f41b-9beb-419b-a804-68a000d9666b][Cayutorme]] Syndicate. The
organisation is understood to be responsible for supplying criminal
groups throughout Imperial space with illegal weapons, and the [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] is
eager to curtail the organisation's activities. To assist with the
operation, Gyriampi [[id:541dcfb3-93ef-40e5-9284-bf5a4f999043][Empire]] League has been authorised to reward any
pilots who help combat the mercenaries.
