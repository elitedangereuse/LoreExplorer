#+title: Concha
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
A concha species that thrives in atmospheres rich with carbon
dioxide. The lip-like upper opening cracks apart to allow a vertical
growth of spiky leaves and bright seeds to stretch upward.
#+end_quote

Concha are a genome of alien flora found on planets and moons with
thin atmospheres across the galaxy. They can be sampled with the
Genetic Sampler and scanned with the Short Range Composition
Scanner. They have a clonal colony range of 150m.
