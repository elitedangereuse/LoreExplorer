#+title: MetaDrive
Hyperdrive-research company based in [[id:2a4e7539-b87c-4ce9-8012-b2690fa3b618][Chi Orionis]] where [[id:4ab3e632-de21-44bc-a834-83b808a737ec][Raan Corsen]] worked and chaired by [[id:9752d776-cd68-44a0-84fe-18132d8b5e74][Femi Dakarai]].

Audited by [[id:68956273-c433-4aac-aac3-1ebe826337e5][BigSix]] in 3302 and later acquired by [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]].
