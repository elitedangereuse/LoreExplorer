#+title: Gyre Tree
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These organisms are characterised by their long, slender limbs,
which often terminate in a large seed pod.
#+end_quote

Gyre Trees are massive plant-like organic structures found in
Notable Stellar Phenomena. They consist of a central core with
twelve symmetrical limbs extending from it, and each limb is forked
in two at the end and tipped with a [[id:93aee7b5-feaa-4f18-ad77-4f159075f51b][Gyre Pod]].
