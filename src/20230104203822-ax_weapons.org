#+title: AX weapons
#+filetags: :Thargoid:
[[id:32267587-a5db-456f-8a09-439ed0309638][Weapons]] designed with the intent to be efficient against Thargoid ships.

The initial AX designs are associated with [[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]]. Later developments include work by [[id:e68a5318-bd72-4c92-9f70-dcdbd59505d1][Azimuth Biotech]] and enhanced designs from [[id:cb71ba02-e47b-4feb-a421-b1f2ecdce6f3][Liz Ryder]] and [[id:baab0645-10f6-4242-998a-b3c899f459a2][Zacariah Nemo]].

The [[id:0ffe3814-d246-41f3-8f82-4bb9ca062dea][Mycoid]] weapon was an earlier biological anti-xeno weapon developed by the [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][INRA]], distinct from modern ship-mounted AX weapons.
