* Trailblazer Fleet Withdrawn, Dodec Starport Revealed

/11 Nov 3311/

[[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] has labelled the withdrawal of its [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer]] megaship fleet as a ‘vote of confidence’ in the self-sustainability of thousands of new [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonies]].

The [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer]] vessels were originally deployed to provide logistical support and essential resources to independent [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] initiatives at the edge of inhabited space and are now being refit for deployment for other purposes.

“The success of the [[id:e7d242a0-17ef-409e-a4b1-0ba261932ed9][Trailblazer fleet]] will leave its mark in the history books of human [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]],” [[id:9b2b1a6d-3645-4776-aeee-4e2db897b988][Mark Rennik]], Chief Operating Officer for [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] commented.

“And now, once the crews have taken a well-deserved rest and the internals are refit, they will continue to serve the [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] in other duties.”

To reinforce the message, [[id:9b2b1a6d-3645-4776-aeee-4e2db897b988][Rennik]] also announced the availability of the newly commissioned [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] [[id:83cc5ab9-e42c-4049-b6ed-081927b0b286][starports]] for [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] efforts, which can be built at any available Tier 3 port locations.

“We’re excited to bring back the classic [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] design. We recognised that the demand for smaller [[id:83cc5ab9-e42c-4049-b6ed-081927b0b286][starports]] meant [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] efforts needed more choice, and the [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] is the perfect solution,” claimed [[id:9b2b1a6d-3645-4776-aeee-4e2db897b988][Rennik]].

[[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] [[id:83cc5ab9-e42c-4049-b6ed-081927b0b286][starport]] permits will be available for premium purchase at usual [[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer]] facilities.

The withdrawal itself had been announced in [[id:bc9f9f05-914d-44b7-89de-09068dcda908][Trailblazer Fleet To Be Withdrawn]].
