#+title: Colony SNB-86
#+filetags: :DataPoint:Settlement:

Colony SNB-86 is an abandoned settlement in the [[id:a25e0462-42fb-48d1-8668-2f9e72994de3][Wredguia JC-K c22-8]] system on body [[https://edgis.elitedangereuse.fr/static/sysmap.html?system=Wredguia+JC-K+c22-8&body_id=12][6 a]].

The location of this site can be found by following the three nearby listening posts located in:
[[id:b955540a-f26b-4b18-8d51-d2cfcea8ba11][Wredguia EB-M c21-11]] / [[id:9069b199-a0c7-49dc-946e-4148ff66ac6d][Wredguia KC-K c22-1]] / [[id:ef5b6a44-acb5-4ddb-ad9c-1547df67d9f7][Wredguia EB-F d11-37]]

* Logs

There are Engineer Materials scattered around the ground, and within the settlement there is a Data Point which may be scanned for an Intel Package, and for Engineer Data.

Around the settlement there are five Settlement Comms Log Uplink points, scanning them gives background to the reason for its abandonment:
