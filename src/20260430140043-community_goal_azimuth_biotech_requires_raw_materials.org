Azimuth Biotech has called on mining vessels to deliver raw materials to
the [[id:4e26dabc-d7bd-4e6b-9619-7afb122f4ce8][Glorious Prospect]] megaship in the [[id:b5d15d69-0f29-4147-b813-07098cf5085f][LHS 157]] system.

The campaign was launched by the corporation's acting CEO, Torben
Rademaker:

"Our mission to continue developing anti-xeno technologies is currently
hampered by a lack of resources to establish new facilities. Azimuth's
investment in the [[id:38143d4a-d8a5-4978-a62c-8b64a0c75969][Proteus Wave]] project and related infrastructure was
considerable. Meanwhile, the reckless actions of anarchists and
political extremists have inhibited our recovery."

"Generous credit payments are being offered for the following mined
metals: cobalt, gallite and osmium. [[id:49673a62-e730-4833-9c29-dd6227e444a0][Shipments]] of these can be delivered
to the [[id:4e26dabc-d7bd-4e6b-9619-7afb122f4ce8][Glorious Prospect]], where they will be processed for a variety of
uses. Dedicated contributors of these freshly mined ores will be granted
a permit to the [[id:a5427e6e-e2f5-4c09-b595-97bbc14894ee][Mbooni]] system, should they not already have it, and
access to our range of hybrid anti-xeno modules at [[id:6083ca63-f129-4999-b775-d25a7d29c36a][Prospect's Deep]]."

As well as credit rewards, the top 75% of contributors will receive the
[[id:a5427e6e-e2f5-4c09-b595-97bbc14894ee][Mbooni]] permit if the initiative is a success.

Azimuth Biotech has set out a seven day campaign, which will end early
on Thursday September 8th 3308.
