#+title: Riri McAllister
#+filetags: :Individual:

Officer in the [[id:17d9294e-7759-4cf4-9a67-5f12b5704f51][Alliance Defence Force]] from
[[id:ff595332-6a13-4f69-ae2f-cc0a0df8e741][Lave]]. She was appointed to the
[[id:b0b347ac-10b8-4190-8787-1557f7d4a6da][Council of Admirals]] and promoted
to rear admiral, with [[id:c51f8115-13d1-4d47-a88a-a126cd66d194][George Varma]]
publicly backing her promotion.

Riri McAllister was later exposed as Nexus, central coordinator and
original architect of the [[id:f5c4ef7c-7a40-4d9f-87e1-904f11efda2f][League of Reparation]]. She framed [[id:c51f8115-13d1-4d47-a88a-a126cd66d194][George
Varma]], was prosecuted by [[id:2569323a-d569-4399-8cda-ae371000dc8b][Mara Klatt]] of [[id:415ecd29-3cf3-4f3c-ab29-a7debb4b56de][Alliance Interpol]], and was shot
by [[id:27665eca-5d5f-4a1d-970d-fb83ce673269][Polly Cartesius]] during her trial on [[id:5c4e0227-24c0-4696-b2e1-5ba9fe0308f5][Alioth]].

Afterwards, McAllister vanished while being transported aboard a
medical ship and was listed as missing rather than confirmed dead.

McAllister claimed to be a direct descendant of
[[id:0be96028-d995-4b52-8bb0-34f21e080bce][John Jameson]], but
[[id:415ecd29-3cf3-4f3c-ab29-a7debb4b56de][Alliance Interpol]] said the claim
could not be verified and that she was registered as an orphan of
unknown lineage.
