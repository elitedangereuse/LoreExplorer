* Winters' Supporters Claim Mining Rush Victory

/15 May 3311/

A surprisingly tense mining boom in
[[id:cf096cc3-59af-46ca-a0ea-a57c4c30c90e][LP 855-34]] has concluded, with
supporters of [[id:b9fe58a3-dfb7-480c-afd6-92c3be841be7][Felicia Winters]]
claiming success over [[id:b4892958-b513-46dc-b74e-26887b53f678][Yuri
Grom]]’s pilots.

A call for [[id:045f7139-ac68-453c-b97f-b61c8a9c370d][Platinum]] by the
system’s governing faction triggered an influx of ships representing the
two powers. Longstanding issues between
[[id:b4892958-b513-46dc-b74e-26887b53f678][Yuri Grom]] and
[[id:d56d0a6d-142a-4110-9c9a-235df02a99e0][the Federation]] fed into the
competitive aspect of the issue, and it is perhaps no surprise that it
was the latter who claimed the greatest number of mined resources in
the end.

Speaking on behalf of Winters’ support base, a representative gave this statement:

“The efforts of our mining teams over the past two weeks have been
nothing short of remarkable. We provided enough
[[id:045f7139-ac68-453c-b97f-b61c8a9c370d][Platinum]] to ensure
that [[id:cf096cc3-59af-46ca-a0ea-a57c4c30c90e][LP 855-34]]’s governing
faction will meet its manifesto building projects. And, just as
significantly, we showed that the
[[id:d56d0a6d-142a-4110-9c9a-235df02a99e0][Federation]] dominates all
competitors in matters of industry!”

Those who took part in the conflict for either side can now collect their rewards.

[[id:b9fe58a3-dfb7-480c-afd6-92c3be841be7][Felicia Winters]] supporters can
collect their rewards at
[[id:999fca61-7f83-45ef-b58f-08355652c7d1][Rhea's Resilience]].

[[id:b4892958-b513-46dc-b74e-26887b53f678][Yuri Grom]] supporters can
collect their rewards at
[[id:4dfda537-e644-4848-b6ec-ad0a3f7cfceb][The Undeniable]].
