* Dodec Station Enters Full Production Availability

/07 May 3312/

[[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] has confirmed that the [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec Station]] has entered full production.

Previously available in limited numbers, the [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] is the largest Tier 3 [[id:83cc5ab9-e42c-4049-b6ed-081927b0b286][starport]] that can be constructed for use in [[id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][colonisation]] efforts.

[[id:d9459015-dae3-4233-9eb7-a2fb11344097][Brewer Corporation]] CEO [[id:0a249cc4-6f5d-4467-bf34-d6419b838581][Madelyn Teague]] issued the following statement to [[id:4ab0f53c-0b85-43a3-83ca-b9e88c0db30e][Vox Galactica]]:

“The [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] is the pinnacle of modern station design. Available with three truss variations and immediate access to a Human Tech Broker, it’s simply the best option available for those looking to colonise new systems and to offer services to all who visit.”

The [[id:4a94cb18-f45e-4484-8798-354b193f8e6a][Dodec]] is now widely available for purchase through [[Id:d1f19609-fdb7-4991-bcb2-e93435f013d0][Colonisation]] contacts.
