* Community Goal: Operation Andronicus

/03 Nov 3303/

With Thargoid attacks becoming a regular occurrence in the [[id:7e99a467-6e3d-418e-8b10-72cf3660efa6][Pleiades Nebula]], [[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]] has leveraged its considerable reserves to fund a military operation in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IR-W d1-55. The initiative, dubbed ‘Operation Andronicus’, has been expressly conceived to counter Thargoid aggression in the region.

Admiral [[id:7bca1ccd-649e-438a-ae56-fb8ca34e6440][Aden Tanner]], [[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]]’s chief military liaison, elaborated on the nature of the initiative:

“Personal-scale craft are particularly effective against Thargoid vessels, not least because of the comparative vulnerability of capital-class ships. I therefore believe the most effective way to address the Thargoid threat – in the short term at least – is a militia of seasoned combat pilots. [[id:85d9e888-3f5b-40ed-b8af-2eb87e42b0d0][Aegis]] has agreed to finance this initiative, which will aim to deliver a strategic blow to the Thargoids in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IR-W d1-55.”

[[id:3808ab0e-fcff-4299-b882-f8c3946f272d][Aegis Research]] has confirmed that it will reward pilots who hand in Pilots [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] [[id:bbbc7bc6-79d7-46b7-925e-c1f882c8f25a][Combat Bonds]] at The Oracle in the [[id:cd15e78b-89d1-4051-bd92-05d0ab49ec17][Pleiades]] Sector IR-W d1-55.

The operation begins on the 2nd of November 3303 and will run for one week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will end immediately.
