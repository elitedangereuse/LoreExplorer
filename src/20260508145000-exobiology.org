#+title: Exobiology
#+filetags: :Odyssey:Concept:

The study and sampling of alien organisms on planetary surfaces, especially Odyssey-era flora species. Independent pilots collect exobiology data using the [[id:cacdbf9c-073f-4a50-ba65-4ab16938c6a4][Genetic Sampler]] and can sell completed results through [[id:91b3e6e1-62aa-4d85-a93a-1d64765a8729][Vista Genomics]].
