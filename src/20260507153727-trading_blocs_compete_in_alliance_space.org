* Trading Blocs Compete in Alliance Space

/22 May 3311/

Two significant trading blocs have emerged in
[[id:1d726aa0-3e07-43b4-9b72-074046d25c3c][Alliance]] territory
following the termination of the Strategic Defence Pact.

Last week,
[[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] was ordered
by the [[id:48ac5ad9-dd0e-4d43-a109-f4cf6d3efdea][Alliance Assembly]] to
reduce its involvement in Allied military infrastructure and logistics.
The move, labelled ‘fascinatingly short-sighted' by Sirius Corp CEO
[[id:f0655b3a-aca9-488f-bdb3-c481a42db384][Li Yong-Rui]], has triggered
an economic rush from Allied corporations to grab the lucrative
contracts issued as a result.

The two trading blocs have somewhat predictably formed around the two
leading voices in the [[id:48ac5ad9-dd0e-4d43-a109-f4cf6d3efdea][Alliance
Assembly]] –
[[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]] and
[[id:0d664f07-640e-4397-be23-6b52d2c2d4d6][Nakato Kaine]].

This marks the latest in a growing series of confrontations between the
galaxy's major Powers. In
[[id:cf096cc3-59af-46ca-a0ea-a57c4c30c90e][LP 855-34]], a contested mining
system, [[id:b9fe58a3-dfb7-480c-afd6-92c3be841be7][Felicia Winters]]
initially secured control by the end of the first week of conflict.
However, her success provoked an immediate and forceful response from
[[id:b4892958-b513-46dc-b74e-26887b53f678][Yuri Grom]]'s supporters.
Conflict zones erupted across the system as Grom's forces pushed back,
ultimately overturning Winters' hold and plunging the region back into
an unoccupied state.

Mahon’s supporters aim to reinforce the Prime Minister’s position as
leader of the [[id:1d726aa0-3e07-43b4-9b72-074046d25c3c][Alliance]] via
high levels of trade. A spokesperson said that Mahon ‘will prevent the
economic disaster which Kaine’s Assembly motion has threatened.’

Meanwhile, Kaine’s supporters claim that ‘the Sirius pact was paper
covering the cracks of Mahon’s economic weakness’, pointed to as
evidence of ‘Mahon’s over-reliance on megacorporate support to get
things done.’ Kaine herself has always advocated for a political shift
towards favouring businesses founded in Alliance member systems.

Both trade blocs have asked for shipments of
[[id:0def679f-6fc4-4107-bd99-51a36668a787][Semiconductors]],
[[id:0b64653e-4cad-4f00-bf49-d88ae6049a11][Ceramic Composites]] and
[[id:f09a0ecd-fc51-40df-ac46-ec179b74ac4e][Synthetic Fabrics]].

Trade pilots looking to help
[[id:da80c263-3c2d-43dd-ab3f-1fbf40490f74][Edmund Mahon]]’s trade bloc can
sign up at [[id:f1db8856-a4de-4cd3-9767-d5e583e5dffb][Bowersox Port]] in
[[id:b9e28958-51d6-4c64-a112-3e84b8fdaf72][Winiama]].

Trade pilots looking to help
[[id:0d664f07-640e-4397-be23-6b52d2c2d4d6][Nakato Kaine]]’s trade bloc can
sign up at [[id:7742cf0e-a818-4fb6-a4f8-9f9622d8efd8][Fuca Dock]] in
[[id:08b61168-83c6-4ef7-a9fc-9d6ccdbf9a3a][Apura]].
