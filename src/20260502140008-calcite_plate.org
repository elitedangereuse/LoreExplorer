#+title: Calcite Plate
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
These structures consist mainly of calcium carbonate.
#+end_quote

Calcite Plates are mineral structures found in Notable Stellar
Phenomena that are produced by colonies of tiny polyp-like
creatures. They cannot be mined.
