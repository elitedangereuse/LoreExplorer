#+title: Teal Nebula
#+filetags: :Region:3312:

A remote nebula in the [[id:edf930ec-8736-45c9-907b-4512626640a8][Empyrean Straits]], holding [[id:f57cff55-3348-45ea-b76f-d0eaa3c68165][Guardian]] ruins catalogued in the [[id:1f552f3e-0cd9-4160-beff-22b15d7eebcd][Eorl Auwsy AA-A H72]] system. Described as among the least explored, it was visited by the [[id:b1b57ce2-5c91-46b6-87aa-c3f1fa29838c][Third Distant Worlds Expedition]] in 3312.
