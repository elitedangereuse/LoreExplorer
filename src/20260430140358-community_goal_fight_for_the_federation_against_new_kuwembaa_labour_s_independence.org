The [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] is fighting to prevent further factions from declaring
independence in the Kuwembaa system.

[[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]] has broken ties with the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] in protest
against the government's civil surveillance programme. Li Chotep for
Equality, a local Federal faction, has been tasked to degrade the
rebelling faction's military strength.
President [[id:02322be1-fc02-4d8b-acf6-9a9681e3fb15][Zachary Hudson]] made a brief public announcement:

"I call upon all Federal auxiliaries to aid in this conflict. The
reputation of the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]] will not be so easily tarnished by these
unlawful separatists."

Two recently seceded factions -- the Coalition of Uteran and Gluscap
Labour -- are considering rejoining the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]], depending on the
result of the Kuwembaa conflict.

Li Chotep for Equality, in partnership with the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]], has offered
credit rewards to any pilots willing to help crush this rebellion by
handing in [[id:bbbc7bc6-79d7-46b7-925e-c1f882c8f25a][Combat Bonds]] at the megaship Fortress Liberty in the Kuwembaa
system.

Should Li Chotep for Equality match tiers with [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]], it
will remain independent but no other factions will secede from the
[[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]].
Should Li Chotep for Equality end one tier ahead of [[id:146b9d9d-6435-404a-a853-0738ccdf8638][New Kuwembaa Labour]],
it will return to being a Federal faction and no other factions will
secede from the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]].
Should Li Chotep for Equality end two tiers ahead of New Kuwembaa
Labour, it will return to being a Federal faction and the Coalition of
Uteran will return to the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]].
Should Li Chotep for Equality end three tiers ahead of New Kuwembaa
Labour, it will return to being a Federal faction and the Coalition of
Uteran and Gluscap Labour will return to the [[id:847b877e-8a00-4e9c-baa3-22f2a071c74b][Federation]].

The two factions have set out seven-day campaigns, which will end when
the top tier is reached by either faction or early on Thursday the 12th
of August 3307.
