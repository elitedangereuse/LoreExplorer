Requires permit to [[id:29b5bb44-c0cd-4f69-88af-a75407b6f40b][HIP 48762]], which is not earnable as far
as anyone knows. The association with [[id:29b5bb44-c0cd-4f69-88af-a75407b6f40b][HIP 48762]] remains a notable oddity.

On 13 Nov 3311, access to the system was granted to licensed [[id:131aabdf-0ed8-4ce2-beec-94c77a8c41d2][Pilots' Federation]] members, as described in [[id:af40c040-ed0a-4d5d-8e0c-291552e7c2dc][Pilots’ Federation Members Enter HIP 87621]]. Civilian, corporate and government traffic remained restricted, and [[id:28dcc8eb-311b-416d-af66-5b9922625d6d][fleet carriers]] were still excluded.

[[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]] remains permit locked, but the permit is now granted to licensed [[id:131aabdf-0ed8-4ce2-beec-94c77a8c41d2][Pilots' Federation]] members.

In 3312, forces aligned with several Imperial and Alliance leaders announced the informal [[id:6fc24809-0ded-4f15-bfd1-c1042b2fd523][HIP 87621 Non-Aggression Pact]] to reduce hostilities across the enclave.
