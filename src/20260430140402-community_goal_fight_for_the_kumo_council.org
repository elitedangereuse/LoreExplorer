The [[id:2dc1b92f-4bdc-4504-ad5f-a9c5794155dc][Kumo Council]] has declared war on the Haithis Purple Dynamic Group
for the latter's role in supporting [[id:d8c4bbec-5bea-43d7-bad6-0ec97600cac3][Neomedical Industries]].

The pharmaceutical corporation's recent campaign in the Haithis system
enabled the mass production of onionhead gamma strain, scheduled to be
available as a medicinal product from [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] and independent markets.
The drug is currently distributed in a handful of anarchy systems by the
[[id:1dfc7c53-642a-4404-a8e8-16a8cc18482a][Kumo Crew]] syndicate.

As hostilities erupted in the Haithis system, [[id:7aae0550-b8ba-42cf-b52b-e7040461c96f][Archon Delaine]] broadcast a
message for [[id:d8c4bbec-5bea-43d7-bad6-0ec97600cac3][Neomedical Industries]]:

"Did you truly believe you could steal from us and survive unscathed?
Now your allies will pay for your greed with their blood."

The [[id:2dc1b92f-4bdc-4504-ad5f-a9c5794155dc][Kumo Council]] intends to prevent the scheduled distribution of
onionhead gamma strain as a medicinal commodity from [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] and
independent markets. Success would keep onionhead gamma strain's supply
limited to anarchy systems selected by the [[id:1dfc7c53-642a-4404-a8e8-16a8cc18482a][Kumo Crew]].

Pilots supporting this effort can deliver combat bonds to The [[id:c72ce3b7-f19e-4034-9df5-554bcddfaa4f][Mictlan]]
megaship in the Haithis system.

The two factions have set out seven-day campaigns, which will end when
the top tier is reached by either faction or early on Thursday the 16th
of September 3307.
