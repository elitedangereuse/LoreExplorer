[[id:1c4d53a2-d81f-4cfa-8001-eb77f2c79b5f][The Alliance]] has announced plans to build an Orbis starport in the
[[id:fd4456a5-20f9-45dc-9d7e-84114b4c251f][Synuefai EB-R c7-5]] system, between the core systems and the [[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]]'s
outposts in the [[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]].

[[id:2506edb2-fef4-4723-85c6-02482635229d][Alliance]] official [[id:eb46eb3f-c399-45fa-a0e3-8362595e24ee][Torvan Dast]] issued the following statement:

"The remoteness of our [[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]] outposts makes reaching them a
potentially dangerous proposition. This new starport will bridge the gap
between the settlements and the galactic core, and lessen the risk to
ships journeying to and from the [[id:6fee136e-381f-47a2-ba13-6d74c8798803][California Nebula]]."

The initiative is being overseen by the [[id:3157a52c-9803-424e-b854-2e2552f07718][LP 128-32 Corporation]], which has
placed an open order for various raw materials for use in the
construction. The organisation has also placed a kill order on all ships
on it's wanted list, to ensure that those contributing material to the
campaign can do so safely.

The campaign begins on the 1st of February 3304 and will run for one
week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will
end immediately.

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before delivering [[id:89bb247d-d459-4ebf-a000-698cd1d9c5fe][Osmium]], [[id:699175f3-27b8-43ca-a463-2896e147de16][Samarium]], and [[id:ccce00cc-b229-4223-9b94-2f8f1e33f925][Lithium Hydroxide]] to Bulychev
Hub in the LP 128-32 system.

Be aware that faction-state changes, UA bombing and other disruptive
events can negatively impact markets and station services, and could
prevent the initiative from running smoothly.
