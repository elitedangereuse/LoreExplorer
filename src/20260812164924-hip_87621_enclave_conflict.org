#+title: HIP 87621 Enclave Conflict
#+filetags: :3311:3312:Alliance:Empire:Federation:Event:

Conflict among Power-aligned forces over the 49-system enclave surrounding [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]], following the discovery of [[id:2025d30d-570c-4b01-bde9-116e0f1e4080][Radicoida unica]]. Large-scale hostilities ended in 3312 under the [[id:cf1770d3-2354-433b-83a6-dc384ea84bfd][October Accords]], which placed HIP 87621 under the custodianship of [[id:1133e15f-59dd-42b3-aaa9-12a0bf3a3287][October Consortium]].
