* Exphiay Entertains Shadow President

/10 Apr 3301/

This evening marks the 153rd annual [[id:735218d7-87f7-4570-8585-9c5ab0288c1d][Exphiay]] Bankers’ Ball. Shadow President [[id:02322be1-fc02-4d8b-acf6-9a9681e3fb15][Hudson]] will be in attendance, as will [[id:d699f206-6c66-4fc3-ba3e-1ce80104993f][Frank Hall]] – CEO of [[id:6bbd1e56-519f-43ad-97fa-44a7e0a09311][Gallant Investment]], [[id:9dd28bad-34fc-413c-b6e5-9603860a915e][Paul Wiggins]] – CEO of [[id:51d2742d-6be5-48ed-b26c-fc9cce4438d0][Wiggins Development Trust]], and [[id:51c64081-63cd-466c-9a30-1a7530ca7f40][Patricia Spellman]] – Vice President of [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] Finance.

The 1 million-credits-a-head dinner will see the Federation’s rich and famous rub shoulders with some of the galaxy’s most influential powerbrokers. One of the main topics of conversation is expected to revolve around fiscal responsibility in the face of the continued failing of the current Federal leadership.  

Notable absences include President [[id:a9ccf59f-436e-44df-b041-5020285925f8][Halsey]] and Vice President [[id:5e4c8ce6-5c18-43c1-ba4b-5bc0e694ca90][Naylor]], both of whom were reportedly not invited to the star-studded event. Further reports indicate that well-known liberal leaning corporations such as [[id:aee128df-b51b-4465-a030-2b8fa2bf2dd3][Better Green Developments]], [[id:2e31258c-b85b-4fb5-b899-5dc4a6b719c9][Roach Investment Banking]], [[id:56b63deb-4395-4a0e-9933-f2ff95a34bf0][Worster Insurance]], [[id:be9fd9dd-f826-446e-bdba-97f8da7359b4][Lycan Moon Mining]] and [[id:0e3c3199-da15-4d55-9166-c1f0c67de845][Benton’s Natural Remedies]] were also all deliberately blocked from attending.
