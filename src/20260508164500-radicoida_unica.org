#+title: Radicoida unica
#+filetags: :3311:Specie:Flora:

Rare [[id:639e4adf-a977-44b2-ad57-dbc51fe83e36][exobiological]] flora discovered in [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]] in 3311.

Reports described Radicoida unica as potentially capable of remaining
dormant for centuries and containing an unusually complex bio-chemical
compound with possible pharmaceutical applications.
