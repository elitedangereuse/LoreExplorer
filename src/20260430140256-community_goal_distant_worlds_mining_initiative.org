The Omega Mining Corporation has requested deliveries of raw materials
for a new Orbis starport, to be built In the heart of the galaxy.

The construction of the starport constitutes part of the ambitious
[[id:f176c68d-cc7a-4db2-8305-81f6771c7e9f][Distant Worlds II]] expedition. Project leader [[id:0250dc51-7007-47d9-92e3-db41de666cbc][Erimus Kamzel]] told the
media:

"The [[id:dbb9956e-5ae2-4c27-94f7-670f0a4719f9][Distant Worlds]] fleet is privileged to be at the forefront of this
ambitious industrial and scientific initiative - humanity's first
starport in the Galactic [[id:9059253d-2c36-4289-9bcf-fd20ba9fc35b][Centre]] region."

Independent miners are invited to deliver shipments of [[id:9e0d3d1a-0515-4718-91e0-514281b9e77f][Indite]], [[id:19346f59-fa1a-4ba2-8373-5b4d90f26662][Gallite]],
[[id:d5d904e1-f1a7-4c46-afe8-728f625d11bf][Praseodymium]] and [[id:3de49bff-8041-45b0-a713-d3646334efd9][Cobalt]] to [[id:c9a8428d-c6ed-4493-859f-0654c5784a5c][Omega Mining Operation]] in the Omega Sector
VE-Q b5-15 system.

The initiative begins on the 17th of January 3305 and will run for one
week. If the final target is met earlier than planned, the campaign will
end immediately.

To be eligible for rewards you must sign up as an active participant
before delivering [[id:9e0d3d1a-0515-4718-91e0-514281b9e77f][Indite]]. [[id:19346f59-fa1a-4ba2-8373-5b4d90f26662][Gallite]], [[id:d5d904e1-f1a7-4c46-afe8-728f625d11bf][Praseodymium]] and [[id:3de49bff-8041-45b0-a713-d3646334efd9][Cobalt]] to Omega
Mining Operation in the Omega Sector VE-Q b5-15 system.

Be aware that faction-state changes and other disruptive events can
negatively impact markets and station services, and could prevent the
initiative from running smoothly.
