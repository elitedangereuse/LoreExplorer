#+title: Reel Mollusc
#+filetags: :Specie:Fauna:

#+begin_quote
These organisms are so called because of their resemblance to reels
or spools, are protected by husks that shield them from radiation.
#+end_quote

Reel Molluscs are non-sapient, spacefaring creatures that inhabit
Notable Stellar Phenomena and loosely resemble the molluscs native
to Earth.
