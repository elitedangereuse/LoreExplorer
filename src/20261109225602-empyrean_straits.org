#+title: Empyrean Straits
#+filetags: :Region:3312:

A higher-stellar-density region of the galaxy entered by the [[id:b1b57ce2-5c91-46b6-87aa-c3f1fa29838c][Third Distant Worlds Expedition]] in 3312 on its way to Waypoint 13. It contains the [[id:f8c7f778-5ad7-478f-9e4b-17c83a7ce2bf][Teal Nebula]] and its Guardian ruins.
