#+title: Q-Type Anomaly
#+filetags: :Specie:Anomaly:

#+begin_quote
A range of phenomena characterised by energetic central orbs.
#+end_quote

Q-Type Anomalies are a a form of Notable Stellar Phenomena. They
react strongly to ships that approach them too closely, and will
emit one of a variety of different energy pulses depending on type.
