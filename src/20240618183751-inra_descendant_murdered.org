* INRA Descendant Murdered

/08 Jun 3304/

A descendant of [[id:ff6c20db-2077-427a-9457-72cb71f7e4f9][Amaro Hem]], one of the [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][INRA]]’s senior members, has been murdered by terrorist group the [[id:f5c4ef7c-7a40-4d9f-87e1-904f11efda2f][League of Reparation]]. His body was found in the wreckage of a crashed ship.

Senior Agent [[id:fb74a286-1688-41e8-9bec-9ef14adaaf1f][Tanya Ramirez]], part of the tri-superpower taskforce investigating the League, provided a full explanation at a press conference:

“We have identified the deceased as [[id:3ccfe121-19d5-4444-b9e8-6a6b5c670c17][Luca Hem]], an unemployed construction worker living at [[id:c6e835f7-0645-4987-9fb6-5650617a5b1b][Houtman City]] in the [[id:8caa6742-32fd-41b6-972c-e5c7db882785][Aeternitas]] system. He was reported missing by his family two weeks ago. We can confirm that he was the great-grandson of [[id:ff6c20db-2077-427a-9457-72cb71f7e4f9][Amaro Hem]], who was a programme coordinator with the [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][INRA]].”

“[[id:3ccfe121-19d5-4444-b9e8-6a6b5c670c17][Luca Hem]]’s body was found strapped into an ancient [[id:83299a14-b6b0-4670-ba39-2914e05ed2f5][Cobra Mk III]], the same model flown by Commander [[id:0be96028-d995-4b52-8bb0-34f21e080bce][Jameson]]. Forensics indicate he was still alive when the ship struck an asteroid in the [[id:8caa6742-32fd-41b6-972c-e5c7db882785][Aeternitas]] A Belt. The Cobra’s derelict condition suggests it wasn’t travelling under its own power, and was most likely salvaged from a scrapyard specifically for this purpose.”

“According to logs discovered at the [[id:2e60a0f2-62a2-49c4-8a3f-45a0669f11fe][Taylor Keep]] [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][INRA]] facility, [[id:ff6c20db-2077-427a-9457-72cb71f7e4f9][Amaro Hem]] was responsible for ensuring that [[id:0be96028-d995-4b52-8bb0-34f21e080bce][Jameson]] did not return from his mission against the Thargoids. This staged murder, recreating the circumstances of Jameson’s death, is obviously intended as an act of poetic justice by the [[id:f5c4ef7c-7a40-4d9f-87e1-904f11efda2f][League of Reparation]].”

“[[id:3ccfe121-19d5-4444-b9e8-6a6b5c670c17][Luca Hem]]’s wife and children have been taken to secure locations for their own protection, as have nine individuals from other families related to [[id:ff6c20db-2077-427a-9457-72cb71f7e4f9][Amaro Hem]].”

Agent Ramirez concluded:

“It’s clear that the [[id:f5c4ef7c-7a40-4d9f-87e1-904f11efda2f][League]] is using theatrical tactics to ensure its message reaches the media. Our taskforce is working hard to discover more about this organisation, and to bring these terrorists to justice.”
