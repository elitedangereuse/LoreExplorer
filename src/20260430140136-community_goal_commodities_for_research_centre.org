Dr [[id:d5ca99a6-ded7-43fb-bc1e-83a622b49c50][Roy Casimir]] of the [[id:3d9b071c-c232-431f-8f63-5c3a594b9909][Holloway Bioscience Institute]] has announced a
research centre focusing on new life forms discovered by the galactic
community:
The development of the [[id:1f629124-e470-420f-ae19-d55c66bb97c5][Codex]] has led to a number of xenological
discoveries. Our aim is to construct a dedicated scientific facility to
analyse these incredible lifeforms.

We therefore request quantities of cobalt, indium and tantalum to be
delivered to [[id:e7277813-d4d3-496c-8fe5-e5f540ff5ef3][Williams Vision]] in the [[id:3efd571e-0167-4167-bf6c-67b4650259ce][Nahuaru]] system. [[id:3efd571e-0167-4167-bf6c-67b4650259ce][Nahuaru]] Crimson
Bridge Int has agreed to fund the initiative and reimburse contributing
pilots.
