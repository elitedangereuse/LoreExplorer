#+title: Taylor Keep
#+filetags: :DataPoint:Empire:Federation:Thargoid:Settlement:

Taylor Keep is an abandoned settlement in the [[id:d15fc8ec-7e9e-41c5-b75c-4c3ce89fd6c2][12 Trianguli]] system, on body [[https://edgis.elitedangereuse.fr/static/sysmap.html?system=12+Trianguli&body_id=5][A 1]].

The location of this facility was given by an [[id:1fd01d13-7a2c-402e-bb92-1b4edadd78c2][Unregistered Comms Beacon]] that can be found in the [[id:d114c07a-da76-4bcd-a3c5-152e2a08e528][Ain]] system.

Taylor Keep was operated by the [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][Intergalactic Naval Reserve Arm]] (INRA), a co-operative between the Federation and Empire that operated during the [[id:c6674165-eb13-47d3-ad54-796aab951892][First Thargoid War]]. This facility appears to have had connections to the project that created the [[id:0ffe3814-d246-41f3-8f82-4bb9ca062dea][mycoid]] used against the Thargoids, operating as a launch point for the delivery of [[id:6023377d-7271-49d1-80ec-ffab82dc8c29][weaponised]] mycoid to a [[id:cf8efef4-6db7-4780-9b79-6321b738adfc][Thargoid hive ship]] by [[id:0be96028-d995-4b52-8bb0-34f21e080bce][Commander John Jameson]].

The facility contains a number of habitation modules, hangar or barracks buildings, it is surrounded by defence turrets. A major feature is the ship landing pad and control tower. There are collectable materials scattered around the site.

Within the facility there are four Data Points which may be scanned for an Intel Package, and for Engineer Data. There is a time limit to scanning the Data Points. The Data Point also will give you the waypoint for the site in the Navigation Panel.
