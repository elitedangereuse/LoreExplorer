#+title: October Accords
#+filetags: :3312:Alliance:Empire:Federation:Event:

Agreement reached in 3312 between [[id:1133e15f-59dd-42b3-aaa9-12a0bf3a3287][October Consortium]] and a coalition of remaining Powers in the [[id:64e3ad83-30b5-41fc-bde5-8eb63c38ab83][HIP 87621]] enclave. It followed the [[id:6fc24809-0ded-4f15-bfd1-c1042b2fd523][HIP 87621 Non-Aggression Pact]], ended large-scale hostilities in the [[id:d4bc10a0-a815-4bb8-8ce4-badb75a95540][HIP 87621 Enclave Conflict]], placed HIP 87621 under the Consortium’s custodianship, and provided for shared scientific access to [[id:2025d30d-570c-4b01-bde9-116e0f1e4080][Radicoida unica]].
