#+title: Anemone
#+filetags: :Specie:Flora:

#+begin_quote
Despite their name, these organic structures more closely resemble
the shells of sea urchins.
#+end_quote

Anemones are a species of Fungal Life that have been found on the
surfaces of airless planets and moons across the galaxy. They
resemble giant Earth-like pumpkins, and were originally nicknamed
"Space Pumpkins" by explorers. They can tolerate a wide range of
temperatures, and are able to absorb energy from nearby stars.
