#+title: Hybrid Drive
#+filetags: :Thargoid:

[[id:d15bc771-25ff-4d76-a48b-d0044ad94e25][Effie Ratling]] developed a prototype hybrid drive at [[id:5caecb15-c8eb-4f3e-8856-db3dd116c017][Almeida Landing]] by applying her research into [[id:a1376710-92d7-4eee-9c26-b36bac8bbf33][wormholes]]. The [[id:39a31dd8-3750-4507-90b7-b649d0eeecef][INRA]] took control of the project and tested a modified prototype; the test pilot did not survive.

Whether this research influenced the later [[id:46a9c980-af48-4e43-a820-9971d7c76c34][Frame Shift Drive]] remains unconfirmed.
