#+title: Radioplankton
#+filetags: :Specie:Lore:Nonsentient:

Radioplankton are tiny, free-floating, carbon-based algae that
inhabit sub-categories of Gas Giant planets called Gas Giants with
Water-based Life and Gas Giants with Ammonia-based Life. Unlike some
other alien species, radioplankton cannot be directly observed, and
are only known to exist through the text descriptions on System
Maps.

Sentience: Non-sentient.
