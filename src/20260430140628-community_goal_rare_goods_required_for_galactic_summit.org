[[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] has requested deliveries of consumables to Luyten's
Star for the forthcoming diplomatic conference.

[[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] has placed orders for these rare commodities: [[id:ba86a2db-c17e-4856-9882-036f95a773c2][Eden]]
Apples Of [[id:20e0bd61-00d4-4492-a53c-44e7ffc054e0][Aerial]], [[id:d80776d9-21ee-42d9-a112-de75ff3ca03d][Ethgreze Tea Buds]], [[id:aa2662c4-12d2-4842-8ff2-ca213e1b1f9e][Gerasian Gueuze Beer]], Giant [[id:76048bd8-7a18-4d77-bf77-ae23a5268d20][Irukama]]
Snails, [[id:53bfde4b-c822-429e-9884-a82c69f124de][Goman Yaupon Coffee]], [[id:2f3b4c97-2fe4-473a-8873-ec2ecababbe7][Karsuki Locusts]] and [[id:5efc49f5-465f-4dd8-afaa-b6bb3595a58c][Lavian Brandy]].

All shipments are to be delivered to Ashby City in the [[id:5817a2f2-d8af-4c78-9401-490abe32564b][Luyten's Star]]
system. They will then be ferried to [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] before the conference
begins.

If the initiative is successful, [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] will make the fully
engineered long range fast boot class 5 [[id:46a9c980-af48-4e43-a820-9971d7c76c34][Frame Shift Drive]] available for
purchase at all human technology brokers.

Each tier reached will further reduce the resources required for
acquiring the module in the first two weeks of availability, starting on
the 26th of February 3307.

[[id:6a46a320-1c53-42d0-bf7d-62c8f9b163ed][Datamined Wake Exceptions]] base requirements 18, reduced by two for each
tier above Tier 1.
[[id:569e8fe8-f5e6-45b3-9248-f90c874f8116][Tellurium]] base requirements 26, reduced by four for each tier above Tier
1.
[[id:1d330101-a83a-44c8-bf9f-c815bca7a379][Electrochemical Arrays]] base requirements 26, reduced by four for each
tier above Tier 1.
[[id:1e6cefd3-feb0-4f28-a913-6be3f20c63dc][Chemical Processors]] base requirements 28, reduced by four for each tier
above Tier 1.

If the initiative is successful, [[id:aae70cda-c437-4ffa-ac0a-39703b6aa15a][Sirius Corporation]] will gift the module
and a permanent [[id:83f24d98-a30b-4917-8352-a2d0b4f8ee65][Sirius]] system permit to the top 75% of pilots. The
module will be placed in storage at the Ashby City starport in the
[[id:5817a2f2-d8af-4c78-9401-490abe32564b][Luyten's Star]] system by the 26th of February 3307.
