* Colonia Celebrations Conclude with Megamarket Opening

/20 Aug 3312/

As anniversary celebrations begin to wind down, Colonia Council issued confirmation that a new megamarket has been established at Jaques Station. 

Magen Wolf, speaking on behalf of the Colonia Council, said "All of our local initiatives were successful, and we intend to recognise the considerable efforts of pilots who travelled all the way to Colonia to help make our 10-year anniversary a triumph." 

Jaques, speaking to Vox Galactica via holo-vid, added: 

"It took me some time to pull in some old favours and negotiate with several corporations, but I have finally established Jaques Station as a megamarket!" 

“This old starport is now proud to offer a full suite of outfitting and service options available. This is a huge boost for Colonia and the region! Every year, our small corner of the galaxy becomes a greater place to visit and stay!" 

Wolf also stated that new tourist beacons would be placed around systems of significance to mark the efforts made by everyone who took part, where visitors “can learn more about the history of Colonia by touring our systems populated by happy colonists.” 

The six beacons can be found in Colonia, Carcosa, Einheriar, Tir, Luchtaine and Blua Eaec WW-E c14-1293.
