* Vista Genomics Seeks Exploration Data From Sanguineous Rim

/02 Jul 3312/

A new exploration initiative from Vista Genomics is aiming to gather data to help assess genetic diversity across the Sanguineous Rim. 

To help celebrate the release of the new ship-launched exploration vessel, Faulcon DeLacy has partnered with Vista Genomics in the initiative, calling on independent pilots to help test the Nomad’s capabilities across extreme terrain. 

Frank Endis, Chief of Design commented in a statement issued to Vox Galactica that “most exploration efforts naturally head towards the centre of the galaxy. We’re keen to help Vista collect more data from systems on the fringes of the Milky Way.” 

Pilots taking part in the initiative will receive a bonus payout for any biological samples found throughout the Sanguineous Rim. 

All interested explorers and exobiologists should head to HD 232819 and register at The Magrathea research megaship before heading out into the black.
