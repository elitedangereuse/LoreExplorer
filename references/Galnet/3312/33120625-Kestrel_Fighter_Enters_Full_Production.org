* Kestrel Fighter Enters Full Production

/25 Jun 3312/

The Kestrel has received the green light for full distribution, having previously been available via limited release only. 

Shipments of Core Dynamics’s latest design have begun to arrive at markets. An accompanying press stated the Kestrel has been developed as a highly capable, dedicated combat ship. 

CEO Owen McKenna remarked: 

“The Kestrel has quickly become the small fighter of choice for combat pilots. We're extremely proud of our engineering and design team.” 

The Kestrel is now available for purchase at shipyards throughout the core systems.
